Mottling usually indicates **decreased tissue perfusion** and **poor circulation** — often a **late and serious sign** of systemic compromise.

### 🩺 **Common causes:**

- **[[Shock]]** (especially **cold/late-stage [[Shock]]** – e.g. septic, cardiogenic, hypovolaemic)
- **Peripheral vasoconstriction** (e.g. in [[❄️ Hypothermia]])
- [[Congestive Heart Failure (CCF)]]
- [[SEPSIS]], [[Septic Shock]]
