**Core problem**
- Extra BVM accessories ↑ **apparatus dead space (VD)**
- Small kids → **very low tidal volume (VT)** → even small VD = major impact
- Result: **CO₂ retention → respiratory failure**

---
**Key equations (know cold)**
- **VT = 6 mL/kg**
- **Physiological VD ≈ 2 mL/kg**
- **Total VD must stay < 40% of VT**
- Practically: **apparatus VD ≈ max 10–15% of VT**

---

**Why this matters**

- Kids already use ~⅓ of each breath as dead space
    
- You add equipment → you “waste” the breath
    
- → less alveolar ventilation → **hypercapnia**
    

---

**Clinical example (memorise pattern, not numbers)**

- 14 kg child → VT ≈ 84 mL
    
- Baseline VD ≈ 28 mL
    
- Add filter + EtCO₂ ≈ +30 mL
    
- → **>50% of breath wasted → unsafe → rapid CO₂ rise**
    

---

**High-risk accessories (approx VD)**

- Filter (BACT-Trap): ~23 mL → **avoid <15 kg**
    
- Catheter mount: ~30 mL → **adult only**
    
- EtCO₂ inline: ~6 mL → **generally safe >10 kg**
    

---

**Age-based simplification**

- **<8 kg (infants):** no accessories
    
- **10–24 kg:** EtCO₂ only (avoid filter unless essential)
    
- **>~30 kg:** most adjuncts acceptable
    

---

**Clinical consequences (testable)**

- False reassurance → normal EtCO₂ but hypoventilating
    
- ↑ work of breathing → fatigue
    
- Poor oxygenation during resus
    
- Delayed hypercapnia → sudden crash
    

---

**Core principle**

- “**The smaller the patient, the less equipment you add**”
    

---

**Infection control (separate but examinable)**

- Pre-opening = **contaminated → do not use later**
    
- Ambulances = high contamination rates (~15–46%)
    
- **Never pre-assemble airway circuits**
    

---

**Bottom line**

- Don’t optimise convenience → optimise **alveolar ventilation**
    
- In paeds airway: **minimal circuit = safest circuit**
