1. **Gastroenteritis**
   - **Definition**: Inflammation of the stomach and intestines, usually due to infection.
   - **Symptoms**: Diarrhea, vomiting, abdominal cramps, fever.
______________________________
1. **Appendicitis**
   - **Definition**: Inflammation of the appendix, often due to obstruction.
   - **Symptoms**: Right lower quadrant pain, nausea, vomiting, fever.
   - **Treatment**: Surgical removal (appendectomy).
________________________________________________
1. **Cholecystitis**
   - **Definition**: Inflammation of the gallbladder, often due to gallstones.
   - **Symptoms**: Right upper quadrant pain, fever, nausea.
   - **Treatment**: Surgical removal (cholecystectomy).
_______________________________________________
1. **Diverticulitis**
   - **Definition**: Inflammation or infection of diverticula in the colon.
   - **Symptoms**: Left lower quadrant pain, fever, changes in bowel habits.
   - **Treatment**: Antibiotics, dietary modifications, possible surgery.
___________________________________________________
1. **Pancreatitis**
   - **Definition**: Inflammation of the pancreas, can be acute or chronic.
   - **Symptoms**: Severe abdominal pain, nausea, vomiting, fever.
   - **Treatment**: Supportive care, pain management.
___________________________________________________
1. **Inflammatory Bowel Disease (IBD)**
   - **Definition**: Chronic inflammation of the gastrointestinal tract (e.g., Crohn's disease, ulcerative colitis).
   - **Symptoms**: Abdominal pain, diarrhea (often bloody), weight loss.
   - **Treatment**: Anti-inflammatory medications, immunosuppressants.
___________________________________________________
1. **Peritonitis**
   - **Definition**: Inflammation of the peritoneum, often due to infection or perforation.
   - **Symptoms**: Severe abdominal pain, tenderness, fever.
   - **Treatment**: Surgical intervention and antibiotics.
