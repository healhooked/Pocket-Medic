Drawer test
- Position patient supine with leg flexed at 90°, patient to keep leg relaxed
- Wrap hands around the proximal tibia, fingers around the back of the knee joint, thumbs on tibial tuberosity
- Either rest forearm down patients’ leg or sit on foot to fix legs position
- Anterior – Pull tibia anteriorly and feel for movement of tibia on the femur (should be no movement, significant movement may indicate anterior cruciate ligament laxity or rupture)
- Posterior – Push tibia posteriorly (should be no movement, significant movement may indicate posterior cruciate ligament laxity or rupture)\
