Examine:
    - Use the **heel of your hand (thenar and hypothenar eminences)** flat against the **left sternal border** (around the 3rd to 5th intercostal spaces).
    - Fingers can be lifted slightly off the chest to better detect upward movement.

- **What you’re feeling for**:
    - A **heave or lift** is a **sustained outward motion** of the chest wall, indicating forceful contraction of a cardiac chamber, usually the **right ventricle**.


Causes:
- **Right ventricular heave** (most common):
    - Seen in **pulmonary hypertension**, **pulmonary stenosis**, or **chronic lung disease** with cor pulmonale.
        
- **Left ventricular heave**:
    - Rarely felt; may be present in **severe aortic stenosis** or **hypertrophic cardiomyopathy** (usually more evident at the apex rather than parasternal).
