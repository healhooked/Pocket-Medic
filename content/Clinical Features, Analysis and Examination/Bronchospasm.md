Constriction of the smooth muscle within the bronchial walls -> narrowing of the airways and increased airflow resistance -> Lower airway obstruction

## ***Clinical Features:***
- [[Wheeze]]
- [[Clinical Features, Analysis and Examination/Dyspnoea|Dyspnoea]]
- Reduced Expiratory Airflow
## ***Cardinal feature:***
- Triggered by) Allergens, exercise, cold air, and chemical irritants & smoke inhalation
- Acute asthma exacerbations 
- Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD)
