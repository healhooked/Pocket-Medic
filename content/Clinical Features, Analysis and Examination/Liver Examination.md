# Palpation:
![[image-5 2.png|303x202]]

- Lay the patient flat.
- Begin palpation from right iliac fossa towards right hypochondrium.
- Ask patient to breath in – liver edge should be felt

Note distance of liver edge to the costal margin (> 1 cm = enlarged - hepatomegaly).
Tenderness may suggest hepatitis or cholecystitis (you may be palpating gallbladder), 
pulsations suggest tricuspid regurgitation

# Percussion:
![[image-4 2.png|289x197]]

- Percuss superiorly of the right iliac fossa until dullness is heard 
- Percuss inferiorly from the 2nd intercostal space until dullness is heard
- Loss of liver dullness may suggest massive hepatic necrosis or free gas in the peritoneal cavity (perforated bowel)
