| **Parameter**      | **Normal Perfusion**              | **Early Shock / Compensated**     | **Late [[Shock]] / Decompensated**     |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **Temperature**    | Warm                              | Cool peripheries                  | Cold extremities                       |
| **Skin**           | Pink, dry                         | Pale, cool, may be clammy         | Mottled, cold, sweaty or very clammy   |
| **Cap Refill**     | < 2 seconds                       | 2–4 seconds                       | > 4 seconds or absent                  |
| **Blood Pressure** | Normal systolic (e.g., >100 mmHg) | Slightly reduced or normal        | Markedly low (<90 mmHg systolic)       |
| **Pulse Pressure** | Normal (30–40 mmHg)               | Narrowing (e.g., <30 mmHg)        | Very narrow or unrecordable            |
| **Pulse**          | Regular, normal rate (60–100 bpm) | Tachycardia (e.g., >100 bpm)      | Bradycardia (late) or very weak/absent |
| **Consciousness**  | Alert and oriented                | Anxious, restless, mild confusion | Drowsy, confused, or unconscious       |
