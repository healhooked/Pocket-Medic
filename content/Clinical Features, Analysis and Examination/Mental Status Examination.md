
| **Mental State Examination (MSE) Component** | **Description**                                                             |
| -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Appearance**                               | Observations about the patient's physical appearance, grooming, and attire. |
| **Behavior**                                 | Assessment of cooperation, eye contact, unusual movements or gestures.      |
| **Speech**                                   | Evaluation of speech rate, volume, coherence.                               |
| **Mood and Affect**                          | Reported mood (e.g., happy, sad) and observed affect (e.g., flat, labile).  |
| **Thought Process**                          | Coherence, logic, and organization of thoughts.                             |
| **Thought Content**                          | Themes and ideas including delusions, obsessions, suicidal ideation.        |
| **Perception**                               | Assessment of hallucinations, illusions, or other perceptual disturbances.  |
| **Cognition**                                | Orientation, attention, memory, abstract thinking.                          |
| **Insight**                                  | Awareness of condition and symptom implications.                            |
| **Judgment**                                 | Decision-making ability and understanding of consequences.                  |
