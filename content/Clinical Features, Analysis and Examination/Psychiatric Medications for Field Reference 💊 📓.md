***Drug Classes:***
### [[SSRIs]](Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitors)

- [[Paroxetine]]: Inhibits the reuptake of serotonin in the brain, increasing serotonin levels.
- [[Sertraline]]: Similar mechanism as other SSRIs, primarily affecting serotonin reuptake.
- Escitalopram

[[Tricyclic Antidepressants (TCAs) & Overdose]]
- [[Amitriptyline]]: Although primarily a tricyclic antidepressant, it also acts as a serotonin reuptake inhibitor.

### [[SNRIs]]  (Serotonin-Norepinephrine Reuptake)
- [[Cymbalta - Duloxetine]]: Inhibits the reuptake of both serotonin and norepinephrine.

### [[Atypical Antipsychotic, Second Generation]]:
- [[Olanzapine]] Acts as a dopamine antagonist and has effects on serotonin receptors.
- [[Risperidone]]: Similar mechanism as Olanzapine, primarily a dopamine and serotonin antagonist.
- [[Clotiapine]] 

### [[Typical Antipsychotic, First Generation]]
- [[Thorazine]] A dopamine antagonist, primarily used for schizophrenia and other psychotic disorders.

### Benzodiazepines

- [[Diazepam]]: Enhances the effect of the neurotransmitter GABA at the GABA-A receptor.
- [[Oxazepam]] Similar mechanism as Diazepam, also enhancing GABA effects.

### Other
- [[Lithium]]: Mood stabilizer, mechanism not fully understood but believed to affect neurotransmitter release and reuptake.
- [[Tramadol]]: A pain reliever that works by inhibiting the reuptake of norepinephrine and serotonin.
- [[Clonadine]]: Alpha-2 adrenergic agonist, primarily used for hypertension and ADHD.
- [[Pregabalin - Lyrica]]: Modulates calcium channels to reduce neurotransmitter release, used for neuropathic pain and seizures.
- [[Droperidol]]Primarily a dopamine antagonist, used for sedation and to manage acute behavioral disturbances.
