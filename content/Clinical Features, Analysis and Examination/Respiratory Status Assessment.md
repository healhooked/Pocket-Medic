
[[Respiratory Rate]]
[[Work of breathing]]

**Chest movement:**
- Equal or unequal rise and fall
- Shallow or deep breathing

**[[Lung Sound Auscultation Sites]]**
- [[Wheeze]]
- [[Crackles]]
- [[Stridor]]
- Absent sounds

**Speech:**
- Full sentences, phrases, words only, or unable

**[[Perfusion Status Assessment (PSA)]]**
 - [[Cyanosis]], [[Pallor]], Diaphoresis

**Consider: [[Mental Status Examination]]**
- Alert, confused, drowsy = potential [[Hypoxia, Hypoxemia]]
- [[Glasgow Coma Scale (GCS)]]

**SpO₂ (oxygen saturation):**
- Normal: >94% (COPD: 88–92%)

**Heart Rate:**
- Tachycardia or hypotension, compensation or decompensation
