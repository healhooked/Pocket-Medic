Kyphosis can impact respiratory function. 
- Mechanical restriction imposed by the spinal deformity, which reduces the space available for lung expansion and impairs the mobility of the rib cage.

**Kyphosis can lead to decreased lung volumes and impaired pulmonary function**. For instance, in patients with thoracic spinal posttubercular kyphosis, corrective surgery has been shown to improve pulmonary function and increase lung volume.[1] This improvement is closely correlated with the degree of kyphosis correction.
