***Onset*** - Was it sudden in onset? Or gradual?
***Provocation*** - Is there anything that makes it better or worse? Inspiration / exhalation.
***Quality*** - Describe the pain? Heavy, stabbing, tingling.
***Radiation*** - Does the pain move anywhere?
***Severity*** - Out of 10, define 10.
***Time*** - What time did it start?
