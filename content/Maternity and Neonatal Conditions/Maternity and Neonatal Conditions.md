---
### 🧠 **Obstetric Olivia’s Field Pearls**
> Quick decision-making under pressure:
- **TWO patients, always.**
   - **Bleeding + pain = red flag**
    
- **Hypertension in pregnancy is never normal**
    
- **Baby not crying? Airway first.**
    
- **Keep neonates warm.** Use mum, blankets, or foil.
    
- **If you see a cord, don’t touch the baby – lift the head off the cord.**
