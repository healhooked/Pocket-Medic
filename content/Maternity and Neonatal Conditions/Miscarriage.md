Spontaneous loss of pregnancy < 20 w of gestation (or less than 400 grams)
20% of pregnancies will end in first trimester miscarriage, with the majority being in the first 8 week

Differentials: 
normal early pregnancy implantation bleed 
ectopic pregnancy 
sexual assault/non accidental-injury

Complications:
- [[Shock]]
- Uterine [[SEPSIS]]

Clinical Features
- Lower (hypogastric) [[Abdominal Pain]]
- Vaginal Bleeding
- Hypotension
- Tachycardia
- Postural Symptoms

Signs of Intrauterine Infection:
- Severe pelvic pain and / or rigidity and / or guarding
- Purulent Discharge
- Fever
