### 🔸 **2. Antenatal Care and Assessment**
- Normal pregnancy progression 
- Maternal risk factors and complications
- Antenatal emergencies (e.g., hyperemesis gravidarum, preeclampsia, gestational diabetes)
- History-taking and communication with pregnant patients
- Maternal vital signs and fetal wellbeing assessment

---

### 🔸 **3. Labour and Birth**

- Stages of labour (1st, 2nd, 3rd stage)
    
- Signs of imminent birth
    
- Pain management during labour
    
- Normal delivery procedures (including infection control and positioning)
    
- Postpartum care and observation
    

---

### 🔸 **4. Obstetric Emergencies**

- Postpartum hemorrhage (PPH)
    
- Shoulder dystocia
    
- Cord prolapse
    
- Breech birth
    
- Uterine rupture
    
- Eclampsia/seizures
    
- Retained placenta
    
- Miscarriage and stillbirth
    
- Preterm labour and birth
    

---

### 🔸 **5. Neonatal Care and Resuscitation**

- Neonatal transition to extrauterine life
    
- APGAR scoring
    
- Assessment of a newborn (tone, color, breathing)
    
- Neonatal resuscitation algorithms (e.g. ANZCOR or NRP)
    
- Airway management (including suctioning, positioning)
    
- Use of BVM and chest compressions in neonates
    
- Thermoregulation and glucose control
    

---

### 🔸 **6. Psychosocial and Ethical Considerations**

- Communication with families in crisis
    
- Cultural sensitivity in maternity care
    
- Parental mental health (perinatal anxiety, postnatal depression)
    
- Consent and refusal of care
    
- Mandatory reporting and child protection
    

---

### 🔸 **7. Medications in Maternity and Neonatal Care**

- Drugs used during labour and delivery (e.g., oxytocin, methylergometrine)
    
- Analgesia and sedation considerations
    
- Medications for neonatal resuscitation (e.g., adrenaline, glucose)
    
- Safety of common paramedic drugs in pregnancy
    

---

### 🔸 **8. Clinical Decision-Making and Transport**

- When and where to transport (home birth, birthing unit, ED)
    
- Time-critical decision-making
    
- Equipment needed for maternity/neonatal calls
    
- Interprofessional collaboration (midwives, obstetricians, NICU staff)
