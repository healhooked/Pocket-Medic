### DSM-5 Criteria for Alcohol Use Disorder

A pattern of alcohol use leading to significant impairment or distress.

Indicated by at least 2 of the following in 12 months:
- Larger amounts or longer use than intended.
- Unsuccessful efforts to cut down.
- Time spent obtaining, using, or recovering from alcohol.
- Craving for alcohol.
- Failure to fulfill major role obligations.
- Continued use despite social/interpersonal problems.
- Reduced social, occupational, or recreational activities.
- Use in hazardous situations.
- Continued use despite physical/psychological problems.

- **Tolerance:** Need for increased amounts or diminished effect.
- **Withdrawal:** Characteristic withdrawal symptoms or use to relieve symptoms.

_________________________
### Severity
- **Mild:** 2-3 criteria
- **Moderate:** 4-5 criteria
- **Severe:** 6 or more criteria

***Clinical considerations for alcoholism:***

- [[Korsakoff's Psychosis]]
- Opthalmoplegia ([[nystagmus]])
