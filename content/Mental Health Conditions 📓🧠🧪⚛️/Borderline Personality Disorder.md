[[Personality Disorder]]


DSM5 5 / 9 requirement for diagnosis
1. Frantic efforts to avoid abandonment
2. Unstable, intense relationships with idealization/devaluation
3. Unstable sense of self
4. Self Damaging impulsivity
5. Suicidal behavour
6. Mood instability
7. Chronic emptiness
8. Inappropriate anger/ poor anger control
9. Stress related paranoia or dissociation

[[Pathophysiology]]
- Multifactoral
- Reduced [[Hippocampus]], [[Prefrontal Cortex]], [[amgydala]] volume (but hyperactive)
- altered connectivity between limbic and prefrontal regions

Neurotransmitter Dysregulation:
- [[Serotonin]] dysfunction - > impulsivity and affective instability
- Dopamine dysregulation -> cognitive and perceptual disturbances
- Altered [[Glutamate]] and GABA systems

HPA Axis Dysregulation:
- Abnormal Stress response patterns
- Altered cortisol regulation

Developmental Factors:
- Early attachment disruptions affecting emotional regulation
- Childhood [[Trauma]] (70-80%)
