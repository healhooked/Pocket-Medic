> [!note] Definition
> **Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder**
> - **[[Neurodevelopmental disorder]]**
> 
> Persistent patterns of inattention and or hyperactivity and impulsivity that interfere with functioning or development.

- DSM criteria: Requires 6 month presentation

[[Pathophysiology]]:
- Dysregulation of dopaminergic and noradrenergic neurotransmission and neuroanatomical pathways, primarily in the [[Prefrontal Cortex]].

***Clinical Features:***
- [[Monoamine Dysfunction]]
- Reduced gray matter volume
- hypoactivity in the frontostriatal and frontoparietal networks
- Dyfunction: basoganglia and limbic system -> impulsvity and emotional dysregulation

Polymorphisms in the dopamine transporters 
	- DAT 1 
	- Receptor DRD4 DRD5 genes.

***Treatment:***
Medications
- Stimulants
- Amphetamines 
- Methylphenidate
- Non-stimulants, atomoxetine, guanfacine
