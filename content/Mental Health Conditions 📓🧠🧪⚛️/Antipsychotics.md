- **Antipsychotics**: Medications for psychotic disorders (e.g., schizophrenia, bipolar disorder).
  
- **Categories**:
  - **Typical (First-Generation)**:
    - Mechanism: Dopamine D2 receptor antagonism.
    - Examples: Haloperidol, chlorpromazine.
    - Side Effects: Extrapyramidal symptoms (EPS), tardive dyskinesia.
  
  - **Atypical (Second-Generation)**:
    - Mechanism: Dopamine and serotonin receptor modulation (e.g., 5-HT2A antagonism).
    - Examples: Risperidone, olanzapine, clozapine.
    - Side Effects: Metabolic syndrome, lower EPS risk.

- **Mechanism of Action**: Modulates neurotransmitter systems (dopamine, serotonin) to restore neural balance.

- **Research Focus**: Novel targets (glutamatergic, inflammatory pathways) for improved efficacy and reduced side effects.
