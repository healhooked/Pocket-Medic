- **Onset**: Symptoms emerge during **infancy or childhood** (before 18 years).
- **Neurobiological Basis**: Involves genetic, epigenetic, and environmental factors leading to altered brain structure/function.
- **Impact**: Impairs areas such as **learning, communication, and social interaction**.

***Examples:***

- **[[Autism Spectrum Disorder]] (ASD)**: Impaired social interaction and repetitive behaviors.
- **Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder ([[ADHD]])**: Inattention, hyperactivity, impulsivity.
- **Intellectual Disability**: Limitations in intellectual and adaptive functioning.
- **Learning Disorders**: Specific deficits in reading, writing, or math.

- [[Schizophrenia]], possibly linked to neurodevelopmental processes
