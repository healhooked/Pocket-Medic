 ***Post‑Incident Family Support***  
    • **No dedicated QAS CPG:** apply Psychological First Aid principles and liaise with Mental Health Liaison Service.

### 🧨 High-Risk Situations
- Acute Suicide Risk
- Intoxicated + Psychotic
- Excited Delirium (controversial; monitor closely)
- Violence to Self/Others
- Drug-Induced ABD (e.g. meth, GHB)
- Postictal confusion vs. behavioral disturbance

### 🧪 Tools & Assessment
- Mental Status Examination (MSE)
- Suicide Risk Assessment (SAD PERSONS / paramedic-adapted tools)
- VIRCA – Capacity Assessment
- Scene Risk Assessment
- QAS CPG: Psychiatric Assessment & ABD

### 💊 Sedation & Meds
- Sedation Protocol (Droperidol 1st line)
- Midazolam (adjunct or seizure-prone)
- Monitor: SpO2, EtCO2, ECG post-sedation
- Consider GHB/mixed tox profile

### ⚖️ Legal & Ethical
- Emergency Examination Authority (EEA)
- Physical Restraint Guidelines
- Refusals: Assess Capacity (VIRCA)
- Mental Health Act: Know jurisdictional requirements

### 💬 Debrief & Support
- Psychological First Aid (for family)
- Mental Health Liaison Pathway
- Culturally Safe Care (especially Aboriginal & Torres Strait Islander patients)
- *Mindful Monique*: Self-care & debrief reminders
