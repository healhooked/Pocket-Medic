> [!note] Identifying Features
> - **Hyperarousal** (↑ NE, amygdala overactivation) → Insomnia, startle response
> - **Re-experiencing** (hippocampal dysfunction) → Flashbacks, nightmares
> - **Avoidance** (PFC-amygdala imbalance) → Emotional numbing, withdrawal
> - **Cognitive/Mood Changes** (serotonin & PFC dysfunction) → Guilt, negative beliefs

***Field Reference Pathophysiology:***
[[HPA Axis]] Dysregulation:
- impaired cortisol feedback -> persistent hyperarousal
- Low cortisol paradoxically increases NA

- [[Amygdala]] Hyperactivity
- [[Prefrontal Cortex]] hypofunction
- [[Hippocampus]] Volume reduction -> memory fragmentation
- [[Monoamine Dysfunction]]

**Treatment:
- [[SSRIs]]
- [[SNRIs]]
- Adrenergic Modulators
- [[Atypical Antipsychotic, Second Generation]] - e.g. [[Risperidone]]
- [[Mood Stabilisers]] - e.g. [[Sodium Valproate]], [[Lithium]]
- [[NMDA]] Modulation - e.g. [[Ketamine]]
