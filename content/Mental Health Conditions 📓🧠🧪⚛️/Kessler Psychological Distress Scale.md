# 🧠 Kessler Psychological Distress Scale (K10)

*Source: Kessler et al., 2003*

> Over the last 4 weeks, how often did you feel the following?  
> Tick one option per row.

---

## ✅ Instructions
- Each item scored:
  - 1 = None of the time
  - 2 = A little of the time
  - 3 = Some of the time
  - 4 = Most of the time
  - 5 = All of the time
- Total score = sum of all 10 items (min 10, max 50)

> Note: If Q2 = "None of the time", skip Q3 (score=1);  
> If Q5 = "None of the time", skip Q6 (score=1)

## ✏️ **Scoring & Interpretation**

| Total Score | Likely level of distress |
|-----------:|--------------------------|
| 10–19 | Likely to be well |
| 20–24 | Likely mild disorder |
| 25–29 | Likely moderate disorder |
| 30–50 | Likely severe disorder |
