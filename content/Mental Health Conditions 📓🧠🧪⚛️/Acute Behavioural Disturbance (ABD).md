### The main principles, where possible, when managing these patient are:

|     |                                                                                                                                   |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| S   | _Safety__:_ person, object, place (POP) threat assessment, constantly, reassess the safety of the patient, paramedics, and others |
| A   | _Aggression__:_ be aware of the common triggers of aggression and violence                                                        |
| F   | _Fix__:_ underlying organic illness, focus on de-escalation strategies                                                            |
| E   | _Evaluate the patient__:_ VSS, PSA, RSA, NSA, SAT score, SAMPLE Hx                                                                |
| T   | _Tactical communication_: active listening, empathy, rapport, influence, behaviour change                                         |
| Y   | _Yes_ I have the right resources: including NT Police, ICP, or other SJA resources                                                |
Triggers for ABD:
Triggers of sudden aggression include:

- _Life or limb_: basic question of self defence against a perceived threat
- _Insult:_ either verbal or physical
- _Family:_ our instincts tell us to protect our nearest and dearest
- _Environment_: when someone encroaches in your personal space
- _Mates:_ aggressive behaviour can be perceived as attractive
- _Order:_ anyone who threatens to disrupt an established system of rules
- _Resources:_ such as money or valuables
- _Tribe:_ we tend to defend those with whom we identify
- _Stopped:_ when you feel ‘stopped’ or obstructed by a situation or person.

2) Finish primary survey

15) Brief the team including the QPS officers
16) Compassionately state the patient is on an [[Emergency Examination Authority (EEA) (EEA)]]

1) Only in the lateral position
2) Record SAT score and vital sign every **5 minutes**

	- GCS
	- SATs
	- Head to Toe when safe
