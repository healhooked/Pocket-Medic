> [!note] Definition
> Structured, time-limited psychotherapy that focuses on the relationship between thoughts, feelings, and behaviours.
> 
> **Core Principles**
> - Identifying and challenging negative thought patterns.
> - Developing coping strategies and problem-solving skills.
> - Behavioral activation to encourage engagement in positive activities.

***Indicated for:***
- [[Major Depressive Disorder]]
- [[Anxiety Disorders]]
- [[OCD 1]]
- [[Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD)]]
- [[Anorexia Nervosa]]
- [[Substance Use Disorders]]
- Sleep disorders (e.g., insomnia)
- Stress management
- Anger management
