| Inhalant            | Typical Types                             | Risks/Effects                                |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| *Volatile Solvents* | Paint thinners, glue, nail polish remover | Neurological damage, respiratory issues      |
| *Aerosol Sprays*    | Spray paints, deodorants, hair sprays     | Skin irritation, respiratory problems        |
| *Gases*             | Nitrous oxide, butane, propane            | Asphyxiation, organ damage                   |
| *Nitrites*          | Amyl nitrite, butyl nitrite               | Headaches, dizziness, potential for overdose |
