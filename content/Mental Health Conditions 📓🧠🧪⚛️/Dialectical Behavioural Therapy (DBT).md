> [!note] Definition
> DBT is a form of cognitive-behavioral therapy that emphasises emotion regulation, mindfulness, and interpersonal effectiveness.
> 1. **Core Components**
>     - Individual therapy sessions.
>     - Skills training groups.
>     - Phone coaching for real-time support.
>     - Consultation team for therapists.
> 


**Skills Focus**
- Mindfulness
![[Distress tolerance]]]]
- Emotion regulation
- Interpersonal effectiveness

### ***Indications for DBT***

- [[Borderline Personality Disorder]] (BPD)
- [[Suicidal Ideation]]
- Emotion regulation issues
- [[Substance Use Disorders]]
- [[Anorexia Nervosa]] & other eating disorders
- [[Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD)]]
- [[Anxiety Disorders]]
