### PCP Summary

- **Type**: Dissociative anesthetic.
- **Mechanism**: NMDA receptor antagonist; affects glutamate and dopamine.
- **Effects**: Dissociation, analgesia, hallucinations, altered perception.
- **Medical Use**: Rarely used today due to side effects.
- **Recreational Use**: Misused for hallucinogenic effects; high risk of toxicity.
- **Toxicity**: Overdose can cause agitation, psychosis, hypertension, respiratory depression.

This concise overview highlights the key points of PCP pharmacology and its uses.
