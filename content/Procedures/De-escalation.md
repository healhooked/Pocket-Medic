1. **Clear Communication**: Use calm, direct language and active listening.
2. **Empathy**: Show genuine concern to build rapport and trust.
3. **Non-Verbal Cues**: Maintain open, non-threatening body language.
4. **Environmental Control**: Minimize stressors like noise and bright lights.
5. **Patience**: Allow time for the individual to express themselves.
6. **Risk Assessment**: Continuously evaluate the individual's behavior and risk level.
7. **Simple Language**: Use straightforward, easy-to-understand statements.
8. **Offer Choices**: Provide options to empower the individual.
9. **Collaboration**: Involve mental health professionals when possible.
10. **Crisis Techniques**: Apply established de-escalation strategies.
11. **Cultural Sensitivity**: Be aware of cultural differences in communication.
12. **Documentation**: Record the interaction and interventions for handover.
