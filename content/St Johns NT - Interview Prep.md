**Teamwork** - Tell me about a time you worked in a team and faced a challenge. What was your role, what did you do, and what was the outcome?
- Chill Out Zone:
The Chill Out Zone is a drug and alcohol service focusing on harm reduction in party precincts on the gold coast. Involving first aid, mental health support and AODs assessment. We were a multidisciplinary team and often worked alongside police and paramedics. This dynamic and challenging work demanded collaborative teamwork, we had to assess the severity of the presentation and plan, facilitate extrication and movement of the client from the street to our facility requiring clear communication and group discussion. Often over radio and multiple stake holders. The results were that we facilited the safe transport and stabilization of the client. Upon reflection, I found that it was the teamwork and communication skills that facilitated the most effective care for the patient.
