**Pulse pressure = SBP – DBP**

- Pulsatile component of blood pressure 
- Marker of the hemodynamic stress exerted on the arterial wall during each cardiac cycle
- Surrogate marker of arterial stiffness, with higher values indicating increased arterial rigidity, particularly in older adults

Narrowed pulse pressure (e.g., <30 mmHg) may indicate low stroke volume, as seen in hypovolemic or cardiogenic shock
 
Widened pulse pressure can be seen in hyperdynamic states or conditions such as aortic regurgitation

Interpretation must account for confounding factors such as arterial compliance, vascular tone, and the effects of vasoactive agents
