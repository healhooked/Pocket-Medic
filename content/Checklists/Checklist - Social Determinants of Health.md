# 🌱 Social Determinants of Health (SDOH) Checklist

Assess if these factors are affecting the patient's health, wellbeing, or care.

---
## 🏠 Economic & Housing
- [ ] Financial insecurity
- [ ] Unemployment or unstable work
- [ ] Housing instability / homelessness
- [ ] Food insecurity
---
## 📚 Education & Health Literacy
- [ ] Low education level
- [ ] Difficulty understanding health information
- [ ] Limited English proficiency
---
## 🤝 Social & Community Context
- [ ] Social isolation / limited support network
- [ ] Experiences of discrimination or stigma
- [ ] Recent loss of a loved one
- [ ] Caring responsibilities (e.g., for children, partner, parent)
---
## 🏥 Access to Healthcare
- [ ] Difficulty accessing services (cost, distance, availability)
- [ ] No regular GP or healthcare provider
- [ ] Lack of transport to appointments
---
## 🌿 Environment & Safety
- [ ] Unsafe home or neighbourhood
- [ ] Exposure to violence, crime, or abuse
- [ ] Poor air or water quality

---
## 🧠 Cultural & Spiritual Factors
- [ ] Language or cultural barriers
- [ ] Religious or spiritual needs affecting care
- [ ] Refugee or migration experience

---
## 📄 Legal & Structural Factors
- [ ] Immigration or visa issues
- [ ] Criminal justice involvement
- [ ] Legal problems affecting housing, employment, or benefits
