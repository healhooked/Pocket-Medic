# ⚠️ Suicide Risk Assessment Checklist
## ✅ 1. Suicidal Ideation
- [ ] Thought about suicide lately
- [ ] Thoughts getting stronger
- [ ] Thoughts in past 24 hours
- [ ] Thoughts often / frequent
- [ ] Thoughts for a long time

---
## ✅ 2. Current Plan
- [ ] Has current plan to take own life
- Planned method: ____________________
- Planned location: ___________________
- [ ] Plan is immediate / next 24 hours
- [ ] Access to lethal means
- [ ] Increased risk-taking (e.g., alcohol/drug use, reckless driving)

---

## ✅ 3. History of Previous Attempts
- [ ] Previous suicide attempt(s)
- Number of attempts: __________
- How long ago: __________
- [ ] Prior psychiatric diagnosis / episode

---

## ✅ 4. Current Stressors
- [ ] Recent relationship breakup
- [ ] Family conflict
- [ ] Job loss / unemployment
- [ ] Abuse / domestic violence
- [ ] Legal issues
- [ ] Chronic pain / illness
- [ ] Grief or loss
- [ ] Trauma
- Other stressor: _____________________

---

## ✅ 5. Protective Factors – People
- [ ] Has support from family / friends / partner / GP / colleagues / services
- [ ] No support identified

---

## ✅ 6. Protective Factors – Personal Coping
- [ ] Has reasons to live
- [ ] Has strategies used in previous crises
- [ ] Has personal strengths
- [ ] No coping strategies identified

---

## 🧮 **Overall Risk & Action**
Count total risk items checked: ____

- [ ] Low risk (0–1)
- [ ] Medium risk (2–3)
- [ ] High risk (4–6)
- [ ] Emergency (4–6 and/or immediate plan)

---

## 📋 **Actions if High / Emergency Risk**
- [ ] Refer to SWSPHN Suicide Prevention Service
- [ ] Complete referral form & get consent
- [ ] Fax to 4623 1796
- [ ] Arrange transport to Community Mental Health Emergency Team or ED
- [ ] Do not leave patient alone
- [ ] Call ambulance (police if patient refuses; complete schedule form)

---

*Reference: NSW Health Framework for Suicide Risk Assessment & Management (2004) + Crisis Support Services (2005) + NDARC (2010)*
