
***✅ Anaphylaxis Recognition Checklist***:

A. Acute Onset Illness WITH:
- [ ] Urticaria, flushing/erythema, and/or angioedema

**AND** involvement of at least one of the following systems:

➤ Respiratory 🫁
- [ ] Dyspnoea (SOB)
- [ ] Wheeze/bronchospasm
- [ ] Stridor or upper airway oedema
- [ ] Rhinorrhoea 

➤ Cardiovascular 🫀
- [ ] Hypotension (> 90 SBP)
- [ ] Syncope or collapse
- [ ] Tachycardia or bradycardia

➤ Gastrointestinal 🤢💩
- [ ] Persistent severe abdominal pain -> *Consider* [[A Clinical Approach to the Abdomen]]
- [ ] Recurrent vomiting
- [ ] Diarrhoea

---

OR

B. Acute Onset of ANY of the following (even ***WITHOUT*** skin involvement):

- [ ] Hypotension (> 90 SBP)
- [ ] Bronchospasm
- [ ] Upper airway obstruction

AND anaphylaxis is considered possible
