> [!note] ECG Features
> - Regular rhythm
> - Rate typically 50-120 bpm
> - Three or more ventricular complexes; QRS duration > 120ms
> - [Fusion](https://litfl.com/fusion-beat-dressler-beat-ecg-library/) and [capture beats](https://litfl.com/vt-versus-svt-ecg-library/)


### Causative Factors and Aitiology:
- **Reperfusion** phase of acute myocardial infarction (MI) — most common cause
- Beta-sympathomimetics such as isoprenaline or adrenaline
- Drug toxicity, especially digoxin, cocaine and volatile anaesthetics such as desflurane
- Electrolyte abnormalities
- Cardiomyopathy, congenital heart disease, [myocarditis](https://litfl.com/myocarditis-ecg-library/)
- Return of spontaneous circulation (ROSC) following cardiac arrest — this probably relates to reperfusion of acute MI as underlying cause of arrest
- [Athletic heart](https://litfl.com/marathon-related-ecg-exasperation/)

**Ventricular Complexes (V):** These are QRS complexes that originate from an ectopic ventricular focus rather than the sinoatrial node. In AIVR, these complexes are typically wide and have a morphology distinct from normal sinus beats, reflecting their origin from the ventricles.

**Capture Beat (C):** A capture beat occurs when a sinus impulse successfully conducts through the atrioventricular node and captures the ventricles, resulting in a QRS complex that resembles a normal sinus beat. This indicates that the sinus node has temporarily overridden the ectopic ventricular rhythm.

**Fusion Beat (F):** A fusion beat is a hybrid QRS complex that occurs when a sinus impulse and an ectopic ventricular impulse simultaneously activate the ventricles. The resulting QRS complex has characteristics of both the sinus and ectopic beats, indicating partial conduction from both sources.

These phenomena are important in diagnosing and understanding AIVR, as they help differentiate it from other ventricular arrhythmias. Fusion beats, in particular, are a hallmark of AIVR and can be observed on an ECG as QRS complexes that are intermediate in morphology between normal sinus beats and ectopic ventricular beats.
