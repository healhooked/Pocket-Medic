Blockade of acetylcholine at muscarinic receptors in the nervous system. 

This inhibition reduces the activity of the parasympathetic nervous system, 
- Dry mouth, 
- Constipation, 
- Blurred vision, 
- Urinary retention,
- Increased heart rate

A simple mnemonic to remember the common anticholinergic effects is **"Can't See, Can't Spit, Can't Pee, Can't Sh*t"**:

- **Can't See**: Blurred vision
- **Can't Spit**: Dry mouth
- **Can't Pee**: Urinary retention
- **Can't Shit: Constipation
