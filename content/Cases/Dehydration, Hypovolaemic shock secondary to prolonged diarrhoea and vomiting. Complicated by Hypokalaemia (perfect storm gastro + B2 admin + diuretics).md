***Describe the Cardiotoxic Effectsof hypokalemia?***
Hypokalemia or low levels of serum potassium is a revesrible cause of cardiac arrest.
Cell: 
- At the level of the cardiac myocyte, reduced extracellular K+ -> memrabne potential depolarization and hyperpoilsation. Impairng Na+/K+ pump ATPase activity accumulating intracellular Na and Ca.
- A key determinantive factor for arrythmogensis.
Tissue:
- These celluular chagnes manifest as variable conduction capacity accross the myocardium, an unstable resting membrane potential. Prolonging ventricular repolaization, presenting as a prolonged QT. Facilitating re-entry circuits due to the shortening of the refractory period. Leading to the febrillation of the myocardium.
System Level:
- A broad spectrum of arrhythmias are associated with hypokalemia, manifesting as AF, AV blocks, VT, VF and torsades.
- ECG include T-wave flattening, ST segment depression, prominent U waves.
