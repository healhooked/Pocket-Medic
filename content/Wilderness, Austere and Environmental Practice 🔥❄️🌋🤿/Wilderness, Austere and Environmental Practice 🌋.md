### 🏕️ **[[Austere & Wilderness Practice]]**

- **Improvised Airway & Bleeding Control** (cricothyrotomy, tourniquet, haemostatics)
- **Long Transport / Extraction Times**
    - Prevent hypothermia
    - Analgesia + immobilisation
    - Reassess often (changing status)
- **Limited Resources**
    - Oxygen rationing
    - Rehydration (oral / rectal routes if IV impossible)
    - Improvise splints, dressings, stretchers
