> [!note] Treatment
> - Warm Environment
> - Remove Wet Clothing
> - Insulate with Blankets
> - Continually monitor Temperature
> 

- **Hypothermia** (Mild → Severe)
    - Passive vs. Active Rewarming
    - Rewarming shock & VF risk


*Consider:*
Three Broad Causes of Hypothermia:
	1. Increased Heat Loss
	2. Decreased Heat Production
	3. CNS Dysfunction
- [[Oxygen]]
- [[12 Lead ECG]]
- [[Blood Glucose Monitoring]] 
- Concentrate on heating for patients with moderate to severe hypothermia
- Administration of warmed [[Sodium Chloride 0.9%]]

__________
*Consider Exacerbating Causes:*
- Overdose
- Substance Use
- [[Hypoglycemia]]
- [[Epilepsy & or Seizure]]
___________

| Hypothermia Presentation |             |                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------ | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hypothermia Stage        | Temperature | Clinical Assessment                                                                                                 |
| Mild                     | > 32 - 35   | Lethargy, Ataxia, Vasoconstriction, Tachycardia, Tachyopnoea, Normotension                                          |
| Moderate                 | >28 - 32    | Confusion, ALOC, Bradycardia, Hypotension, Muscle Rigidity                                                          |
| Severe                   | < 28        | Stupor or coma, Diminished or absent signs of life, Apnea, Dilated Pupils, Reduced or absent reflexes, Dysrhythmias |
