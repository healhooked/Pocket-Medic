#### 💧 Water & Hydration
*Purification:*
Boil water with a pot or metal cup.
Use improvised charcoal/sand bottle filters.

>![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bOKlW3M7vFY&ab_channel=ClayHayes)

Use iodine drops, bleach (2 drops per liter).
Oral rehydration solution (ORS): 6 tsp sugar + 0.5 tsp salt per liter of clean water.

Prevent dehydration: Shade shelters, rationing, sweat-minimizing movement.

---
#### 🔥 Heat & Cold Injury Management
Heatstroke: Strip down, soak clothes, fan air, immerse in stream if possible.
Hypothermia: Create "hypo-burrito" using emergency blankets, dry clothes, sleeping bags, plastic sheets.
Rewarming: Skin-to-skin, warm rocks, water bottles, heated water in plastic bags.
Shelter: Trash bags, emergency bivvies, ponchos, tarps with cordage.

---
#### 🕸️ Improvised Airway/Breathing Aids
- Jaw thrust/chin lift: Maintain airway manually if no adjuncts.
- Improvised NPA: With small tubing (controversial, last resort).
- Improvised barrier mask: Plastic sheet with breathing hole if you must give mouth-to-mouth.
- Chest seal: Use plastic (e.g., chip bags) taped on 3 sides.

---
#### 🧠 Neurologic & Mental Health in Isolation
- Managing panic attacks or anxiety: Grounding, breathing techniques.
- Delirium or altered mental status: Prioritize hydration, warmth, and glucose.
- Group psychology: Prevent group panic, encourage routine and rest.

---
#### 🧰 Surgical/Field Procedures with No Kit
> Only if trained

Abscess drainage: Heated knife, sterilized blade.
Amputation or field surgery: Extreme last resort.
Needle decompression: Only with sterile sharp + understanding of landmarks.

#### 🐍 Bites, Stings & Allergies without Meds
Snakebite: Pressure immobilization using elastic band or long stretch bandage.

Anaphylaxis without EpiPen: 
- Desperate cases may require stimulants (e.g. caffeine), but this is not safe—you need evacuation.
Bee sting removal: Scrape with card, sterilize with alcohol or heat.
Swelling: Ice packs (improvised with cold stream water and sealed bags).

---
#### 🚑 Transport Without Gear
Improvised litters: Between two branches with jackets, sleeping bags, or tarps.
>![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xNA_UwsMSgQ&ab_channel=SixEcho)

Human carry: Fireman’s carry, piggyback, two-person seat carry.
Drag tarp: Groundsheet or rainfly drag sled.

---
#### 🧭 Remote Navigation, Signaling & Rescue
*Signaling:*
Smoke, mirror flashes, bright clothing.
SOS with rocks, logs, or in the snow.
Improvised mirrors: Phones, CDs, sunglasses.
Night rescue: Reflective tape, strobes, flashlight blinking patterns.

---
#### ⚗️ Medical Kit Hacks
Tampons = Nasal packing or wound filler.
Condoms = Water storage, occlusive dressing, glove substitute.
Duct tape = Blister protection, wound closure, splinting aid.
Dental floss/paracord = Sutures, lashing.
