# Examine:
### Tone
- Ask patient to keep arms relaxed
- **Shoulder** – circumduct (“roll”) shoulder
- **Elbow** – flex/extend
- **Wrist** – circumduct (“roll”) wrist
- Look for:
    - **Spasticity** – tone ↑ with speed (stroke)
    - **Rigidity** – tone constant despite speed (Parkinson’s)
- Assess bilaterally
### Power
Active Resistance of the:
- **Shoulder**
    - Abduction (C5)
    - Adduction (C6/C7)
- **Elbow**
    - Flexion (C5/C6)
    - Extension (C7)
- **Wrist**
    - Flexion (C6/C7)
    - Extension (C6)
- **Fingers**
    - Abduction (T1)
- Assess bilaterally
- Precision Grip
### Reflexes
- **Biceps** (C5/C6) – thumb over tendon, tap
- **Supinator (brachioradialis)** (C5/C6) – tendon at posterolateral wrist, tap
- **Triceps** (C7) – tap above elbow at 90° flexion
- Assess bilaterally

- **Hyperreflexia** → UMN lesion (stroke, SCI)  
- **Hyporeflexia** → LMN lesion (plexus/peripheral nerve injury)
### Sensation
![[image-18 3.png|670x362]]
**[[Dermatome sites]]** (avoid boundaries):
- C5 – lateral deltoid
- C6 – palmar thumb
- C7 – palmar middle finger
- C8 – palmar little finger
- T1 – medial antecubital fossa
**Tests:**
- **Light touch** – cotton wool, sternum for demo
- **Pinprick** – neuro-tip, sternum for demo
- **Proprioception** – thumb up/down with eyes closed
- Assess bilaterally

- Pattern recognition: nerve damage, neuropathy, spinal cord lesion
### Coordination
- **Finger-to-nose test**
    - Fully extend to examiner’s finger then nose
    - Repeat quickly, bilaterally
    - Look for dysmetria / intention tremor → ipsilateral cerebellar pathology (CVA)
### Dysdiadochokinesia
- “Clapping game” (alternate pronation/supination fast)
- Assess bilaterally
- Slow/irregular → ipsilateral cerebellar pathology (CVA)
