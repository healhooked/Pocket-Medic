____________________
- **Rigidity** – constant involuntary reflex contraction associated with tenderness – note location (peritoneal irritation/inflammation) (peritoneal irritation / inflammation)
- **Guarding** – voluntary resistance when palpation occurs due to contraction of abdominal muscles – note location (tenderness, anxiety)
- **Rebound tenderness** – Pain worse when hand is released – note location (peritoneal irritation/inflammation)
- **Masses** – large or superficial masses – note location, size, shape, mobility, pulsatility (hernia, enlarged organs, cysts, tumour)
- **Pulsations**, [[Abdominal Aortic Aneurysm (AAA)]]
