# Palpation:
- Palpate from the right iliac fossa towards left hypochondrium
- As patient breaths out, he splenic edge may be felt
- The splenic edge should not be felt under normal physiology
- If felt the spleen is felt, it is potentially 3 x larger than normal (splenomegaly)

# Percussion:
- Percuss from right iliac fossa towards left hypochondrium
- No dullness
- Dullness may indicate splenomegaly
