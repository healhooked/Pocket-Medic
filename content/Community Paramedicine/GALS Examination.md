
# History

- **Musculoskeletal history:** RA, gout, other MSK conditions.
- **Symptoms:** Painful/stiff joints, red/swollen joints, back or neck pain (acute/chronic).
- **Red flags:**
    - Cancer history, fevers, chills, weight loss (malignancy).
    - Saddle anaesthesia, bladder/bowel dysfunction (cauda equina).
    - Persistent pain >4 weeks, unrelieved by treatment.
- **Other:** Rash (dermatomyositis), Raynaud’s phenomenon, functional difficulty (buttons, stairs, washing).
# General Examination

- **Environment:** Aids (slings, splints, walking aids, wheelchairs, shower/toilet rails).
- **Appearance:** Body habitus, scars, muscle wasting, psoriasis, deformities, asymmetry.
- **Front:** Posture, shoulder alignment, joint swelling, muscle bulk.
- **Side:** Cervical lordosis, thoracic kyphosis, lumbar lordosis, knee hyperextension, foot arch.
- **Back:** Spinal alignment (scoliosis), iliac crest alignment, popliteal swelling (Baker’s cyst), Achilles tendon thickening.
# Gait

- Ask patient to walk a short distance (with aids if normally used).
- Observe Abnormalities of:
    - Gait cycle: heel strike/toe-off.
    - Discomfort, limping, reduced ROM.
    - Leg length discrepancy.
    - Turning (slow = restriction/instability).
    - Abnormal gait: 
	    - [[Trendelenburg gait]]
	    - [[Waddling gait]]
	    - [[Parkinsonian Gait]]
    - Footwear: worn soles suggesting asymmetry.

# Upper Extremities

![[fig-2-full.png|456x229]]
**Position:** Patient standing.
#### Passive ROM (both arms, observe pain/crepitus)

- **Shoulders:** Forward flexion, abduction/adduction.
- **Elbows:** Flexion/extension.
- **Wrists:** Flexion/extension.
#### Active ROM (both arms, observe pain/crepitus)

- **Shoulders:** Abduction, adduction, flexion, extension.
- **Elbows:** Flexion, extension, pronation, supination.
- **Wrists:** Flexion, extension, radial deviation, ulnar deviation.
- **Hands:**
    - Small joint flexion (fist).
    - Grip strength (reduced in pain, LMN lesions).
    - Precision grip (thumb–finger opposition).
## Inspection
- Dorsum of hand: asymmetry, swelling, deformity.
## Special Test
- **[[MCP squeeze]]:** Pain = inflammatory arthropathy

# Lower Extremities

**Position:** Patient supine.
#### Passive ROM (both legs, observe pain/crepitus).

- **Hips:** Flexion, extension, abduction, adduction, internal/external rotation.
- ![[image-20 1.png|401x263]]
- **Knees:** Flexion, extension, hyperextension.
- ![[image-21 1.png|418x173]]
- **Ankles:** Dorsiflexion, plantarflexion, inversion, eversion.
- ![[image-22 1.png|352x176]]
#### Active ROM (both legs, observe pain/crepitus)

- **Hips:** Flexion, adduction, abduction, internal/external rotation.
- **Knees:** Flexion (0–140°), extension (180°).
- **Ankles/feet:** Dorsiflexion, plantarflexion, inversion, eversion, toe flexion/extension.
#### Special Test

- **[[MTP squeeze]]:** Pain = inflammatory arthropathy
