### **History**
- Diagnosed neurological conditions?
- Stroke or head injury?
### **Symptoms**
- Fits / faints / “funny turns”
- Weakness, numbness, clumsiness, balance problems
- Sensory issues: vision, hearing, smell, balance
- Headaches:
    - Sudden/severe → subarachnoid haemorrhage
    - Fever/chills/neck stiffness → meningitis/encephalitis
    - Increasing frequency/intensity → space-occupying lesion
    - With focal signs → CVA or lesion
    - Temporal tenderness → temporal arteritis
- Recent trauma → haemorrhage risk
- Sleep difficulties
- Mood: sadness, depression, anxiety (“nerves”)
- Memory changes
