**Whooley Two Item -Depression/Anxiety Screening**:
During the past month:
- Have you often felt down, depressed, or hopeless?
- Have you felt little interest or pleasure in doing things (anhedonia)?

**Questioning**:
- **Biological** history / Family history / Psychosocial history
- **Psychological** – Neurovegetative questions, Lifestyle, Health literacy, Cognitive decline, Mental and AODs problems
- **Sociological** – Social determinants, Basic & Instrumental ADLs, Social Isolation
