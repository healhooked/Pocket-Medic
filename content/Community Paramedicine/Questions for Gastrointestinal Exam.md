### **History**
- GI disease / surgery / CVD (adhesions, mesenteric ischemia)
- Alcohol use
- Medications (antibiotics, steroids, opioids)
- Pregnancy
### **Diet / Eating**
- Swallowing difficulty
- Heartburn / indigestion
- Diet/appetite changes
- Unintentional weight loss
### **Bowels**
- Normal habits?
- Constipation / diarrhoea / urgency / incontinence
- Stool change (loose, hard, black, red, fatty)
- Passing wind?
- Straining?
### **Nausea / Vomiting**
- Onset, timing after meals
- Vomit description:
    - Fresh blood (MW tear / varices)
    - Coffee ground (ulcer)
    - Undigested food (stricture / pouch / achalasia)
    - Non-bilious (pyloric obstruction)
    - Bilious/faecal (lower GI obstruction)
### **Other**
- Pain (intermittent/constant, meal-related, posture-related)
- Abdominal bloating / distension
