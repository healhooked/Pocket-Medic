**RED FLAGS**

**Red flags** **are symptoms that require a high priority. Patients with red flags should not be left at home and should have a same day referral to either a GP or an ED.** 

## Special groups

Even in the absence of red flags, the following patients should have a lower threshold for ED transfer or same day GP referral:
·       Abnormal vital signs
·       Multiple comorbidities
·       Multiple medication (>5), or poor medication compliance
·       Extremes of age
·       Pregnant
·       Immunocompromised, prolonged steroid use, chemotherapy, radiation or HIV
·       Previous cancer
·       Very socially isolated and vulnerable

## Constitutional

These global and nonspecific symptoms should also have lower threshold for ED transfer or same day GP referral:

·       Unexplained weight gain or loss?
·       Unexplained fever, chills, or night sweats?
·       Unexplained and prolonged fatigue?
