# General Inspection

- **[[Vital Sign Survey (VSS)]]**
- **Environment:** Vomit bowls, fluids, medications, catheters, feeding tubes, stomas.
- **Appearance:** [[Cachexia]], [[Obesity]], pain, agitation, lethargy, posture (foetal, knees flexed).
- **Skin:** Jaundice, striae.
- **Scars, bloating.**
# Face and Neck
## **Eyes:** 
- [[Eye lid Xanthelasmata]]
- [[conjunctival pallor]]
- [[Jaundice]]
## **Mouth:**
- [[Angular cheilitis]]
- [[Oral candidiasis]]
- [[Ulcers]]
- [[Glossitis]]
## **Neck:** 
- [[Acanthosis nigricans]]
- [[Lymphadenopathy]] 
# Chest

- [[Gynaecomastia]]
- [[Spider naevi]]

# Abdomen, Back, Pelvis
## Abdomen:
**Position:** Patient supine.
### Inspection
- [[Abdominal Distention]] (fat, fluid, obstruction).
- [[Striae]]
- [[Dilated veins]] (portal hypertension).
- [[Hernias]] (on [[Cough]]).
- Scars, masses.

![[Physical Examination of the Abdomen]]
### Special Tests
- [[Psoas sign]]
- [[Obturator sign]]

# Upper Extremities
- [[Jaundice]]
- [[Koilonychia]] 
- [[Leukonychia]] 
- [[Palmar erythema]] (chronic liver disease).
- Dupuytren’s contractures (alcohol, diabetes).
- [[Finger clubbing]].
- [[Asterixis]] (hepatic encephalopathy, uraemia).
- [[Needle track marks]]
- [[Purpura]] 
- [[Petechiae]] (thrombocytopenia, anticoagulants).
