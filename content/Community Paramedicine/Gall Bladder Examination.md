# Palpation:
- Deeply palpate the midclavicular line, right costal margin
- Ask the patient to breath out then take a deep breath
- Gall bladder will appraoch the hadn
- If pain is elicited, this is a postiive Murphys sign, may indicate [[Cholecystitis]]
