### **History**
- MSK condition diagnosed?
- Rheumatoid arthritis or gout?
### **Symptoms**
- Joint pain / stiffness
- Joint redness / swelling
- Back or neck pain (acute vs chronic)
    - Red flags: cancer history, fever/chills/weight loss, sensory loss (legs/groin), bladder/bowel change, persistent >4wks, not relieved
- Rash (dermatomyositis)
- Fingers painful/white/blue in cold (Raynaud’s)
- Function: button shirt, climb stairs, hang washing (ADLs)
