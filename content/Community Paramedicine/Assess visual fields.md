Visual fields
- Remove patients’ glasses, sit directly opposite patient – about 1m away
- Ask the patient to cover one eye with their hand, focus on part of your face (ie. nose), and not to move head or eyes
- Mirror patient – cover eye, focus and same part of face, do not move head or eyes
- Position finger at equal distance between you and the patient, starting peripherally and moving centrally (assess all four quadrants)
- Ask patient to report when they first see fingers
- Repeat with the other eye

If you can see fingers, and patient can’t, this may suggest decreased visual field (scotoma, tunnel vision, monocular vision loss, stroke, tumour, abscess)
