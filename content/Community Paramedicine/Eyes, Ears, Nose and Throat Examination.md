# Eyes
### General Inspection
- Peri-orbital: swelling, redness, discharge, proptosis.
- Pupils: size, shape, symmetry.
- Eyelids: ptosis (Horner’s, CNIII palsy).
- Sclera: yellow (jaundice), blue (osteogenesis imperfecta), red (scleritis/iritis).
### Pupillary Reflex
- Shine pen torch, observe constriction.
- Repeat both eyes.
### [[Fundoscopy]]
- Optic disc
- Retina , quadrants, shape colour contour
- Macula - flame heamorrages,
# Ears
### General Inspection
- Hearing aids, mobility aids.
- Pinnae: asymmetry, deformity, scars, lesions.
- Mastoid: swelling, erythema, scars.
- Pre-auricular region: pits, lymphadenopathy.
- Conchal bowl: erythema, discharge.
### Hearing Tests
- **Gross Hearing :** Whispered voice test.
- **Weber’s Test:** Lateralisation (normal, conductive, sensorineural).
- **Rinne’s Test:** AC > BC (normal/positive), BC > AC (conductive loss).
### [[Otoscopy]]
- Technique: straighten canal, gentle insertion.
- **Canal:** wax, erythema, discharge, foreign body.
- **Tympanic Membrane:** colour, shape, perforation, scarring.
# Nose
### General Inspection
- Skin lesions, deformity, epistaxis.
- Nasal cavity inspection with torch/otoscope.
### Palpation
- Bones, cartilage alignment/tenderness.
- Infraorbital ridges (fracture).
### Airflow Assessment
- Occlude one nostril, compare airflow bilaterally.
# Throat
### General Inspection
- Face swelling.
- Lips, tongue, teeth, gums, mucosa.
- Parotid duct, palate, uvula, arches, tonsils, floor of mouth.
### Bimanual Palpation
- External & internal palpation.
- Parotid, submandibular, sublingual glands.
- Assess any lumps.

###  BPPV Assessment & Treatment
**Dix-Hallpike Test**
- Contraindications: neck/back problems.
- Position supine with head extended & rotated 45°.
- Look for nystagmus/dizziness.
- If positive → perform **Epley manoeuvre**.

**Epley Manoeuvre**
- Sequential head rotations & patient roll.
- Hold each position for 30–60s.
- Can repeat 2–3 times.
