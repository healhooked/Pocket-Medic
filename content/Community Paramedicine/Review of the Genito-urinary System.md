### **Urinary Symptoms**
- Fluid intake normal?
- Urine: volume, colour, odour (dark = red flag)
- Dysuria / burning / pain
- Flank pain or tenderness
- Urine stream: weak, delayed start, dribble at end
- Nocturia (getting up at night)
- Polyuria (passing more than normal)
### **Other**
