### *Field Reference - Pathophysiology*
Atiology - allegens + irritants -> IgE dependent inflammatory response -> Chronic inflammation
- [[Bronchoconstriction]]: Smooth muscle contraction around the airways leads to narrowing.
- Airway Hyperresponsiveness: Exaggerated response to stimuli (e.g., allergens, cold air).
- Mucus Production: Increased mucus secretion obstructs airflow.
_________________
1. Increased [[Work of breathing]] 
	+ + airway resistance and (-) pulmonary compliance -> hypercapnic respiratory failure
2. [[V/Q mismatch]] – Airway narrowing and closure -> impaired gas exchange -> + WOB to compensate
3. ++ Increase venous, due to (d/t)  ++ intrapleural pressures, increased afterload -> [[Pulsus paradoxus]]

### *Triggers*
- Specific:[[URTI]], allergen exposure (housemite, pollen, animals, [[Aspirin]])
- Non specific: cold air, exercise, atmospheric pollutants, stress
