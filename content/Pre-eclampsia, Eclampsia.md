- Multisystem Disorder
- >20 weeks of gestation with new-onset hypertension 
- (systolic blood pressure ≥140 mm Hg or diastolic blood pressure ≥90 mm Hg) and one or more signs of organ involvement. 
_______________________
System Involvement:
• **Renal involvement**: 
- Proteinuria (spot urine protein/creatinine ratio > 30 mg/mmol), elevated serum creatinine (≥90 µmol/L), or oliguria (<80 mL/4 hours).
• **Hematological involvement**: 
- Thrombocytopenia (<100,000/µL), hemolysis, or disseminated intravascular coagulation (DIC).
- Hypertension
- Oedema
• **Liver involvement**: 
- Severe epigastric or right upper quadrant pain
- Jaundice
• **Neurological involvement**: 
- Persistent headache
- Visual disturbances (e.g., photopsia, scotomata), or convulsions (eclampsia).
- Hyperreflexia
- Clonus
• **Pulmonary involvement**:
- [[Acute Pulmonary Oedema (APO) & ARDS 💦🫁]]
• **Fetal involvement**: Fetal growth restriction (FGR)
___________________

**Eclampsia**
- new-onset [[Medical and Toxicological Conditions 🌿🧪🫁🫘/Epilepsy & or Seizure|Epilepsy & or Seizure]] in a woman with preeclampsia
- Seizures can be tonic-clonic, focal, or multifocal and may occur antenatally, intrapartum, or postpartum, usually within 24 hours of delivery but occasionally later.

***Complications***
- placental abruption, 
- DIC
- cerebral haemorrhage
- hepatic failure 
- acute renal failure

**Risk factor**s 
- primigravida 
- history of pre-eclampsia 
- gestational hypertension 
- extremes of maternal age 
- renal disease 
- diabetes 
- obesity 
- family history 
- multiple pregnancies

The Royal Australian and New Zealand College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists and the Society of Obstetric Medicine of Australia and New Zealand (SOMANZ) provide guidelines for the diagnosis and management of these conditions.
