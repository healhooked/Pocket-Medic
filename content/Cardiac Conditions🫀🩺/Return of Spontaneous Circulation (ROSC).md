1. If pulse present, check response (AVPU)
2. Continue with 5 step approach:  
    1. **Airway -**
        - specifically to see if patient still tolerating and any fluid/vomitus in airway that may need to be suctioned. 
    2. **Breathing -**
        - specifically RR and TV and implement ventilation strategy based on these: 
            - EtCO2 range 30 to 40 mmHg (see CPG)
            - SpO2 > 94% to 98%
    3. **Circulation -** 
        - specifically BP, and correct with up to 1 L NaCl if required (listen to lungs to be prudent). 
    4. **BSL and Temp** (part of H's and T's)
    5. **12 Lead ECG** (part of H's and T's)

Patients **with a pulse following ROSC** if hypoventilating for whatever reasons:
    - Adults: 
        - With basic or SGA in place, ventilate at 10 to 12 breaths per minute (**1 breath every 5 to 6 seconds**) to achieve the following parameters: 
            - **EtCO2 30 to 40 mmHg** (reason is to reverse acidosis from resp and metabolic causes as a result of prolonged anaerobic respiration from the arrest)
            - SpO2 > 96%
        - See - [CPG/Resuscitation: Post ROSC caresLinks to an external site.](https://www.ambulance.qld.gov.au/docs/clinical/cpg/CPG_Post%20ROSC%20management.pdf)
            - The CPG says 8 to 12 breaths per minute which works out to be 1 breath every 5 to 7.5 seconds. 
            - The modified range above is easier to remember and fits within the parameters of the CPG (who can remember 7.5 seconds?). 
    - Paediatrics: 
        - With basic or SGA in place, ventilate at 12 to 20 breaths per minute (**1 breath every 2 to 3 seconds**, possibly even quicker than this) to achieve the following parameters: 
            - **EtCO2 30 to 40 mmHg** (reason is to reverse acidosis from resp and metabolic causes as a result of prolonged anaerobic respiration from the arrest)
            - SpO2 > 96%
                - This recommendation is not found in any of the local CPGs, however is supported by international recommendations. 
                    - [AHA - Tropijan - 2020 - Part 4: Pediatric Basic and Advanced Life Support: 2020 American Heart Association Guidelines for Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation and Emergency Cardiovascular CareLinks to an external site.](https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/10.1161/CIR.0000000000000901)  
                        - Treatment of Respiratory Failure - Treatment of inadequate breathing with a pulse 
                            - _For the ease of training, the suggested respiratory rate for the patient with inadequate breathing and a pulse has been increased from 1 breath every 3 to 5 seconds to **1 breath every 2 to 3 seconds** to be consistent with the new CPR guideline recommendation for ventilation in patients with an advanced airway._
5. Patients with a pulse, **NOT** following ROSC, if hypoventilating for whatever reasons: 
    - Same as above, except only change is that it's a normal EtCO2 range that is being aimed for of **35 to 45 mmHg.**

## 🫁 ROSC Ventilation & Post-ROSC Management

| **Focus Area**           | **Details**                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Airway**               | • Confirm patient still tolerating airway (e.g. SGA) <br>• Suction fluid/vomitus if required <br>• Consider ETI if needed                                                                                                         |
| **Breathing**            | • Ventilate: <br>  – Adult: 10–12 bpm (1 breath every 5–6 s) <br>  – Paediatric: 12–20 bpm (1 breath every 3–5 s; AHA: every 2–3 s) <br>  – If no EtCO₂: ventilate at 8–12 bpm <br>• Maintain EtCO₂ 30–40 mmHg <br>• SpO₂ >94–98% |
| **Circulation**          | • Aim SBP >100 mmHg (adults) <br>• Aim SBP >80 mmHg (children) <br>• Correct BP with up to 1 L NaCl (check lungs first) <br>• Consider adrenaline if hypotension persists                                                         |
| **Disability**           | • Assess responsiveness (AVPU) <br>• Monitor BSL and temperature (part of H's & T's)                                                                                                                                              |
| **ECG / Rhythm**         | • 12-lead ECG: identify reversible causes and treat dysrhythmias <br>• Optimise for ventilation & perfusion                                                                                                                       |
| **Other considerations** | • Consider posturing <br>• Maintain normothermia <br>• Continue 5-step approach (Airway, Breathing, Circulation, BSL/Temp, ECG)                                                                                                   |
