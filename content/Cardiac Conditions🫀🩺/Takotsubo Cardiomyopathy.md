> [!Clinical Features]

***Field Reference Pathophysiology***
- Acute stress -> catecholamine surge ->  microvascular spasm
- Apex of the left ventricle (+++ sympathetic fibres) -> regional wall dyskinesia -> [[Left Ventricular Outflow Tract Obstruction (LVOTO)]]
