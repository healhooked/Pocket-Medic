- 1. **Malignancy**: This is the most common cause, particularly due to metastatic cancers such as lung cancer, breast cancer, and lymphomas.[1-3]
- 2. **Infection**: This includes bacterial, viral, and tuberculous pericarditis.[2-3]
- 3. **Idiopathic**: Cases where no specific cause is identified.[2-3]
- 4. **Iatrogenic**: Complications from medical or surgical procedures, including cardiac surgery, catheterization, and pacemaker insertion.[2-3]
- 5. **Uremia**: Associated with chronic kidney disease and dialysis.[2-3]
- 6. **Connective Tissue Diseases**: Conditions such as systemic lupus erythematosus and rheumatoid arthritis.[3]
- 7. **Trauma**: Both blunt and penetrating chest injuries.[3-4]
- 8. **Post-Myocardial Infarction**: Including free wall rupture and Dressler's syndrome.[2-3]
