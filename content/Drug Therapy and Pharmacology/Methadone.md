**Mechanism of Action:**
- **Full Opioid Agonist**: Activates mu-opioid receptors to relieve pain and reduce withdrawal symptoms.
- **Long Half-Life**: Provides sustained effects, helping to stabilize individuals in treatment.

**Indication:**
- Used for the treatment of opioid use disorder and for managing moderate to severe pain.

**Drug Class:**
- **Opioid Agonists**.

**Side Effects:**
- Nausea
- Constipation
- Drowsiness
- Respiratory depression
- Sweating
- Potential for dependence and overdose
