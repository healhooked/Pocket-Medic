***Indicated for***
[[Meningococcal Meningitis]] [[SEPSIS]] (with a non-blanching petechial AND/OR purpuric rash)
Acute upper GI bleeding (with a history of liver disease or oesophageal varices)

***Contraindications***
- Allergy : Cephalosporin Antibiotics
- Known immediate OR severe hypersensitivity to
penicillin OR carbapenem based drugs

Relative contraindications (requires consultation
with the QAS Clinical Consultation & Advice Line):
- Patients less than 1 month of age
