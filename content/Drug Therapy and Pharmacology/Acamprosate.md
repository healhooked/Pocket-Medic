Calcium Salt
Glutamate Modulator

**Indication:**
alcohol dependence in individuals who are abstinent and seeking to maintain their sobriety.

**Mechanism of Action:**
- Modulates glutamate release to reduce cravings.
- Enhances GABA activity to promote calming effects.
- Restores balance between excitatory and inhibitory neurotransmission.

**Side Effects:**
- Diarrhea
- Nausea
- Abdominal pain
- Headache
- Fatigue
