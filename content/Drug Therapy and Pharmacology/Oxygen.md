---
### 🩺 **Clinical Considerations**

- Always **titrate to effect** and reassess frequently.
- **COPD or CO₂ retainers**, **Paraquat toxicity, Cystic Fibrosis, Neuromuscular Disease:** Aim for **SpO₂ 88–92%** to avoid hypercapnia.
- If **SpO₂ < 85%**, escalate to **emergency response** and consider advanced airway support.

- **BVM**: Use with airway adjunct (e.g., oropharyngeal airway); two-person technique ideal.
- **LMA**: Insert if unable to intubate or BVM is ineffective.
- **ETT**: Confirm placement with EtCO₂, chest rise, auscultation, and CXR.
- **[[CPAP]]/[[PEEP]]**: Watch for [[Hypotension]]; contraindicated in facial [[trauma]], low GCS.

- Ensure **non-rebreather reservoir is inflated** before applying to patient.
- For nebulised medications, use **air-driven nebuliser if oxygenation is not needed**, or supplement O₂ as needed based on baseline SpO₂.
