***Pharmacology:*** 
- Serotonin reuptake inhibitor [[SSRIs]]
- Noradrenaline reuptake inhibitor [[SNRIs]]
- Histamine H1 blockade
- Alpha 1 blockade (orthostatic [[Hypotension]])
- Muscarinic M1 Blockade ([[Anticholinergic]] effects)
- Sodium channel blockade (cardiac sodium channels)

***Side Effects:***
- Unintended sedation (muscarinic and H1 receptors)
- Weight Gain (5HT, Nor Adr Reuptake)
- Orthostatic [[Hypotension]] and Tachycardia (alpha 1 receptors)
