D2 5HT2a

- **Classification**: [[Atypical Antipsychotic, Second Generation]]
    
- **Indications**:
    
    - Treatment of [[Schizophrenia]]
    - Treatment of bipolar disorder (manic or mixed episodes)
    - Management of irritability associated with autistic disorder
- **Mechanism of Action**:
    
    - Risperidone works by antagonizing (blocking) dopamine D2 receptors and serotonin 5-HT2A receptors in the brain. This dual action helps to balance the levels of these neurotransmitters, which can alleviate symptoms of [[Psychosis Disorders]] and mood disorders.
- **Common Side Effects**:
    
    - Weight gain
    - Sedation
    - Extrapyramidal symptoms (e.g., tremors, rigidity)
    - Increased prolactin levels (which can lead to menstrual irregularities or sexual dysfunction)
- **Dosage Forms**: Available in oral tablets, orally disintegrating tablets, and injectable forms (long-acting).
