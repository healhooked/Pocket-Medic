[[Congestive Heart Failure (CCF)]], [[Chronic Kidney Failure]]
- Conservative administration, due to fluid restrictions
- Risk of [[Acute Pulmonary Oedema (APO) & ARDS 💦🫁]]

[[Traumatic Brain Injury 🧠]]
- **Permissive hypotension is contraindicated**
- Target - SBP between 100–120 mmHg

Uncontrolled [[Hemorrhage 🩸]]
- Administer enough fluids to maintain bilateral radial pulse pressure

[[Traumatic Spinal Cord Injury 🧑🦽 1]]
- Cautious fluid administration
- Risk of neurogenic [[Acute Pulmonary Oedema (APO) & ARDS 💦🫁]]

[[Inferior STEMI]], Right ventricular Infarction
- Gentle Fluid challenge
- Only in cases of no signs of left ventricular failure (e.g. APO)

Serial NIBP for all Patients 10-20 m intervals



| Condition                                       | Fluid Management Strategy                                                  | Reasoning / Notes                                                                                                       |
| ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **[[SEPSIS]] (non-hemorrhagic)**                | - Aggressive initial fluid resuscitation (e.g. 30 mL/kg crystalloid bolus) | To counteract distributive shock and restore perfusion. Monitor for fluid responsiveness.                               |
| **[[Burns]]**                                   | - Formula-guided resuscitation (e.g. Parkland formula)                     | High fluid loss through evaporation and capillary leak. Balance risk of over-resuscitation (compartment syndrome, APO). |
| **[[Dehydration / Hypovolaemia]]** (non-trauma) | - Fluid replacement with isotonic crystalloids                             | Restore circulating volume and perfusion. Monitor for signs of overload, esp. in elderly or cardiac patients.           |
| **[[Diabetic Ketoacidosis (DKA)]]**             | - Gradual fluid resuscitation over time                                    | Rapid shifts can cause cerebral oedema. Replace deficits over 24–48 hrs.                                                |
| **[[Pre-eclampsia / Eclampsia]]**               | - Restrictive fluid administration                                         | Risk of pulmonary oedema due to endothelial dysfunction. Monitor closely.                                               |
