Opioid Antagonist

**Mechanism of Action of Naltrexone:**
- **Opioid Receptor Antagonist**: Blocks mu-opioid receptors in the brain.
- **Reduces Pleasurable Effects**: Diminishes the rewarding effects of alcohol. By antagonistic pathway.
- **Decreases Cravings**: Helps lower cravings for alcohol by inhibiting endogenous opioid effects.

_________________________


**Side Effects of Naltrexone:**
- Nausea
- Headache
- Dizziness
- Fatigue
- Anxiety
- Insomnia
- Liver enzyme elevation (with high doses)
