**Indications:** 
- Major Depressive Disorder
- Generalised Anxiety Disorder
- Neuropathic Pain

#### **Mechanism of Action**

A. Serotonin-Norepinephrine Reuptake Inhibitor (SNRI)  
1. Inhibition of serotonin, noradrenaline reuptake  
2. Increased serotonin and noradrenaline  levels in the synaptic cleft   
3. Enhanced noradrenergic, and serotonin neurotransmission  

B. Modulation of Pain Pathways  
3. Interaction with descending pain pathways  
4. Role in the treatment of chronic pain conditions

#### ***III. Side Effects Related to Mechanism***

A. Serotonin-related Side Effects  
1. Nausea and gastrointestinal disturbances  
2. Sexual dysfunction (e.g., decreased libido, anorgasmia)  
3. Serotonin syndrome (rare but serious)  
B. Norepinephrine-related Side Effects  
4. Increased blood pressure and heart rate  
5. Anxiety or agitation  
C. Other Common Side Effects  
6. Drowsiness or fatigue  
7. Dry mouth  
8. Sweating
