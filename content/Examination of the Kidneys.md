**Ballots**:
- Place one hand just below the costophrenic angle
- Place the other hand under the patients flank
- Ask the patient to take a deep breath to try to capture the kidney between your hands
- Repeat on the other side

The kidney may not be ballotable in healthy individuals, may be ballotable in low BMI patients on inspiration
Enlarged kidneys (hydronephrosis) may be cased by kidney stones, blood clots, scarring, tumors, enlarged prostate, pregnancy, UTI, kidney infection.
