## 🩺 <span style="color:#2E86C1;">Pregnancy & Birth Periods</span>

| Term                      | Definition                                                                                             | Key Points                                                                                  |
|-------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Antepartum (Prenatal)** | Period before childbirth.                                                                             | Antenatal visits, ultrasounds, screen for GDM & preeclampsia.                               |
| **Intrapartum**         | Period during labor and delivery.                                                                       | Monitor labor, manage complications (fetal distress, uterine rupture).                      |
| **Postpartum (Postnatal)** | First 6 weeks after childbirth.                                                                      | Watch for PPH, infection, thrombosis; support recovery & newborn care.                      |

## 👶 <span style="color:#27AE60;">Pregnancy History Terms</span>

| Term            | Definition                                                                                                      | Key Points                                                                                  |
|----------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Gravidity**  | Total number of pregnancies, regardless of outcome.                                                               | Includes live births, miscarriages, ectopics, abortions.                                    |
| **Parity**     | Number of pregnancies ≥20 weeks, regardless of outcome.                                                           | Used in obstetric history.                                                                  |
| **Nulliparous** | Woman who has never given birth.                                                                                 | —                                                                                           |
| **Primiparous** | Woman who has given birth once.                                                                                  | —                                                                                           |
| **Multiparous** | Woman who has given birth two or more times.                                                                     | —                                                                                           |

## ⚠️ <span style="color:#C0392B;">Complications & Clinical Signs</span>

| Term                  | Definition                                                                                                 | Key Points                                                                                  |
|---------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Ectopic Pregnancy** | Embryo implants outside uterine cavity (usually fallopian tube).                                           | Pain, amenorrhoea, bleeding; manage with methotrexate, expectant, or surgery.               |
| **Uterine Rupture**  | Tearing of uterine wall during pregnancy or labor.                                                          | Risk: prior C-section, high parity; emergency surgery required.                             |
| **Post-Vaginal Bleeding** | Bleeding after pregnancy or between pregnancies.                                                       | Assess onset, duration, nature; causes: miscarriage, ectopic pregnancy.                     |
| **Shoulder Tip Pain** | Referred pain at shoulder tip from diaphragmatic irritation via phrenic nerve.                             | Seen in intra-abdominal bleeding: ectopic pregnancy, uterine rupture.                        |
| **Kehr's Sign**     | Left shoulder pain when lying down with legs raised.                                                          | Suggests diaphragmatic irritation (e.g., free blood in peritoneum).                          |

## 🌸 <span style="color:#AF7AC5;">Reproductive Milestones</span>

| Term         | Definition                                                                                                         | Key Points                                                                                  |
|------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Menarche** | First menstruation, usually ages 11–14.                                                                           | Marks onset of reproductive capability.                                                     |
| **Menopause** | Permanent cessation of menstruation, usually ages 45–55, after 12 months amenorrhoea.                             | Symptoms: hot flashes, night sweats; hormone replacement may help.                          |
