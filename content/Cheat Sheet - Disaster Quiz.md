# 🔑 CORE FORMULA (MEMORISE)

> **Risk = Hazard × Exposure × Vulnerability ÷ Capacity**

- ↑ Hazard = ↑ Risk
- ↑ Exposure = ↑ Risk
- ↑ Vulnerability = ↑ Risk
- ↑ Capacity = ↓ Risk

---

# 📚 DEFINITIONS (EXAM WORDING)

**Disaster** = Serious disruption exceeding a community’s capacity to cope.

**Hazard** = Potential damaging event.  
**Exposure** = People/assets in harm’s way.  
**Vulnerability** = Susceptibility to harm.  
**Capacity** = Ability to cope & recover.  
**Resilience** = Ability to absorb, adapt, transform & thrive.

---

# 🌍 DISASTER TYPES

### Natural

- Geophysical – Earthquakes
- Hydrological – Floods
- Meteorological – Storms
- Climatological – Drought
- Biological – Pandemics
### Man-made
- Technological – Industrial accidents
- Environmental – Pollution
- Societal – War, terrorism

⚠️ Modern disasters = multi-hazard & cascading.

# 🔄 DISASTER MANAGEMENT CYCLE

| Phase            | Focus             | Example                  |
| ---------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------ |
| **Mitigation**   | Reduce risk       | Build stronger hospitals |
| **Preparedness** | Get ready         | Drills, stockpiling      |
| **Response**     | Save lives        | EMS, shelters            |
| **Recovery**     | Restore & improve | Rebuild better           |

⚠️ Stockpiling = Preparedness  
⚠️ Building codes = Mitigation
