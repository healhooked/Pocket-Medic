> [!note] Treatment
> Stabilise Flail Segment
> ***Consider:*** [[Endotracheal Intubation (ETT)]]
> [[Oxygen]] titrated to presentation
> [[Analgesia]]
> Treat associated injuries

***Complications:***
- [[Pneumonia]]
- [[Pneumothorax 🫁]]
- Actelectasis
- [[Respiratory Conditions🫁🌬️💨⚕️/Respiratory Failure]]

***Field Reference - Pathophysiology***
Fracture of two or more adjacent ribs in two or more places with loss of chest wall stability
- Paradoxical movement of the chest wall -> respiratory distress

Associated w. haemothorax, pneumothorax, pulmonary contusion.
