### Our Philosophy:
- **Diversity of Resources -** Not one document contains the all, including state/jurisdictional ambulance service documents
- **Rationality -** Clinical practice ought to be grounded in rational consistency
- **First Principals Analysis** - Clinical thinking is most effectively understood through a reductionist lens, beginning with its fundamental principles and building upward from there.

*Starting with the physical sciences and biochemical phenomena, we advance to physiological processes, which inform system-based presentations and patient complaints. Effective practice arises from considering each analytical aspect and the emergence of the patient's presentation.*

- **No time for fluff, stack shocks!** - Emergency paramedicine is all about high-yield practice! What is the most efficient, effective and resource-conscious route to the best patient outcome
- **No hierarchical nonsense** - Your contribution and knowledge should be valued no matter the colour of your uniform or epaulette. Fresh eyes are fresh eyes! You don't need to be a paramedic or a doctor to understand science or humanistic practice. We believe in a flat hierarchy.
