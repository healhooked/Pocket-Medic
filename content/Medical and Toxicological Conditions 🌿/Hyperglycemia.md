*Presentation*
*Neurological* - *[[GCS 1]]?*
- Lethargy
- ALOC
- [[Epilepsy & or Seizure]]
- Coma
*Cardiovascular*
- Signs of hypovolemia (hypotension, tachycardia)
- Pale, cool or clammy
- Flushed, hot if febrile

[[Kussmaul Respirations 1]] - severe metabolic acidosis
____________________
*Aetiology of DKA and HHS*
- [[Acute Coronary Syndrome (ACS)]]
- [[Stroke & or Transient Ischaemic Attack]]
- [[SEPSIS]] & Infection
- Incorrect medication management

    -> increased hepatic gluconeogenesis  
    -> increase in glucagon, cortisol, catecholamines
