**Field Reference - Pathophysiology:**
- **Type 1 Diabetes**: Autoimmune destruction of pancreatic **β-cells** → ↓ insulin production → [[Hyperglycemia]]
- **Type 2 Diabetes**: **Insulin resistance** + progressive **β-cell dysfunction** → inadequate insulin response → [[Hyperglycemia]]
*A nuanced understanding of diabetes reflects the grey area between type 1, and type 2 
____________________
- Both types lead to **chronic glucose accumulation in blood**, causing long-term **vascular and organ damage**
