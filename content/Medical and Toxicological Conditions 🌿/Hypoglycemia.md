> [!note] ***Treatment:***
> - [[Glucose ORAL]]
> - [[Glucose 10%, IV]]
> - [[Sodium Chloride 0.9%]]
> - ***Consider:***
> - [[Glucagon]] IM

***Presentation***
*Autonomic Features* 
- Hunger
- Tingling around the mouth
- Anxiety
- Diaphoresis 
- Tremors 
- [[Tachycardia]] 
- [[Pallor]] 
- [[Palpitations]] 
Autonomic features can be masked by [[beta blockers]]

*Neurological features* 
- [[Headaches & Migraines]]
- Dizziness
- Lethargy
- Changes in behaviour (e.g. agitation)
- Visual disturbances
- Slurred speech
- Coma
- [[Epilepsy & or Seizure]]
