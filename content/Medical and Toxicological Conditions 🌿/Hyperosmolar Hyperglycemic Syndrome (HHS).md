***Treatment***
[[Sodium Chloride 0.9%]]
[[Oxygen]]
***Extended Scope:***
[[Insulin (Short-acting – Actrapid®)]]

***Clinical Considerations:***
• BGL: DKA (> 10 mmol/L) AND/OR HHS (> 40 mmol/L)
• Kussmaul respiration is due to the severe metabolic acidosis. This is not usually seen in HHS.

***Presentations:***
***Neurological:***
• Lethargy
• ALOC
• Seizure
• Coma

***Cardiovascular:***
• Signs of hypovolaemia (hypotension, tachycardia)
• Pale, cool or clammy or
• Flushed, hot if febrile.
_____________________
*Aetiology of HHS* - *Consider Associated Treatment Pathways*
- [[Acute Coronary Syndrome (ACS)]]
- [[Stroke & or Transient Ischaemic Attack]]
- [[SEPSIS]] & Infection
- Recent Surgery
- Poor medication management
_________________

- three times less frequent than DKA
- - deaths often due to co-morbid conditions (MI)
- higher mortality rate than DKA
- part of a continuum with DKA, with insulin resistance predominant over insulin deficiency

**Field Reference - Pathophysiology:**
> glycosuria + osmotic diuresis  
-> just enough insulin to prevent lipolysis and ketone production

- RESP – tachypnoea
- CNS – drowsy, delirium, coma, focal or generalised seizures, visual changes, hemiparesis
