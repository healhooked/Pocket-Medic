*Causes*
   Dopamine blockade (typically from antipsychotics).
  - Imbalance between dopamine and acetylcholine in the [[Basal Ganglia]].
  
*Symptoms*
- Sustained muscle contractions.
- Abnormal postures.
- Oculogyric crisis (involuntary eye movement).
- Torticollis (twisted neck).

*Management*
- Anticholinergic medications (e.g., [[Atropine]]))
- Diphenhydramine (antihistamine) as an alternative treatment.
