***Cognitive Assessment and Screening***
➤ Quick Orientation Check (A&Ox4):
- Person: “What’s your name?”
- Place: “Where are we?”
- Time: “What’s the date/day?”
- Situation: “Do you know why we’re here?”

➤ Simple Recall Test (Modified 3-word recall):
- Say: “I’ll tell you three words – apple, car, penny. I’ll ask you to remember them.”
- After 1–2 minutes: “What were the three words I asked you to remember?”

Identifies acute confusion, delirium, or suspected cognitive impairment.

---

🔹 2. Collateral History is Key
Gather info from:
- Family/carers
- Aged care facility staff
- Medical records, if available

Ask:
Is there a formal dementia diagnosis?
What is the patient’s baseline?
Any recent changes in cognition, behavior, or function?

Any prescribed dementia medications (e.g., donepezil, memantine)?



---

🔹 3. Behavioral Observations

Note:

Confusion, repetition, disorientation

Inappropriate or withdrawn behavior

Difficulty following instructions

Wandering or unsafe actions



---

🔹 4. Functional Assessment

Can the person:

Communicate clearly?

Safely walk or transfer?

Take medications correctly?

Identify emergency contacts?


These clues help determine risk and disposition.


---

🔹 5. Consider Delirium vs. Dementia

Feature	Delirium	Dementia

Onset	Sudden (hours–days)	Gradual (months–years)
Course	Fluctuates	Steady decline
Attention	Impaired	Often preserved
Consciousness	May be altered	Normal until late
Cause	Often reversible (infection, drugs)	Progressive brain disease


If a known dementia patient suddenly worsens, think delirium until proven otherwise (e.g., UTI, sepsis, dehydration, hypoxia, polypharmacy).


---

🔹 6. Use of Prehospital Tools

No gold-standard dementia-specific tool is validated for prehospital use, but you can use:

✅ ISBAR or IMIST-AMBO Handover:

Include dementia status in the “Assessment” or “Medical History” component:

“Pt is known to have moderate Alzheimer’s. Baseline is forgetful but ambulant and communicative. Today is acutely confused with no known precipitant.”


✅ 6-Item Screener (Modified for quick use):

Year?

Month?

Age?

Repeat 3 objects (e.g., ball, tree, book)

Count backward from 20 to 1

Recall the 3 items


Score ≤4 suggests cognitive impairment. Takes under 1 minute.


---

🧠 Summary: Prehospital Dementia Assessment

Step	What to Assess

1.	Orientation & simple recall (A&Ox4 + 3-word recall)
2.	Collateral history (baseline cognition, meds, Dx)
3.	Observe behavior (confusion, agitation, risk)
4.	Functional capacity (safety, mobility, communication)
5.	Delirium screening (sudden vs gradual onset)
6.	Clear documentation & handover (ISBAR, IMIST-AMBO)
