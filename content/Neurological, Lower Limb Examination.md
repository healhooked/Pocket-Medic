# Gait
- **Normal walking** – stance, base, stability, arm swing, stride length, step height, turning
- **Tandem gait** (heel-to-toe) – reveals subtle ataxia
- **Tiptoe walking** – tests ankle plantarflexion (S1/S2)
- **Heel walking** – tests ankle dorsiflexion (foot drop)
- **Types of gaits**
    - Ataxic – broad-based, unsteady (cerebellar, sensory ataxia)
    - Parkinsonian – shuffling, stooped, reduced arm swing, hesitation
    - High-stepping – often due to foot drop
    - Waddling – trunk sway, proximal weakness (myopathy)
    - Hemiparetic – stiff leg arcs out (post-stroke)
    - Spastic paraparesis – bilateral hemiparetic pattern (HSP)
## Proprioception
- **Romberg’s test** – feet together, eyes open then closed
    - Loss of balance with eyes closed = sensory ataxia

## Tone
- Patient semi-recumbent, legs relaxed
- **Hip** – rotate in/out
- **Knee** – lift/lower with foot on bed
- **Ankle** – circumduct and dorsiflex quickly

- Look for: 
    - **Spasticity** – tone ↑ with speed (stroke)
    - **Rigidity** – tone constant regardless of speed (Parkinson’s)
- Assess bilaterally
## Power
- **Hip**
    - Flexion (L1/L2)
    - Extension (L5/S1/S2)
- **Knee**
    - Flexion (S1)
    - Extension (L3/L4)
- **Ankle**
    - Dorsiflexion (L4/L5)
    - Plantarflexion (S1/S2)
- **Toes**
    - Big toe extension (L5)
- Assess bilaterally
## Reflexes
- **Knee-jerk** (L3/L4) – tap patella tendon
- **Ankle-jerk** (S1) – tap Achilles tendon
- **Plantar** (L5/S1) – scrape sole of foot
    - Normal = flexion of toes
    - Abnormal = Babinski sign (extension of big toe, fanning)
- Assess bilaterally
- **Hyperreflexia** → UMN lesion (stroke, SCI)
- **Hyporeflexia** → LMN lesion (plexus or peripheral nerve injury)
## Sensation

**Dermatome sites** (avoid boundaries):
- L1 – inguinal region, medial thigh
- L2 – anterior middle/lateral thigh
- L3 – medial thigh above knee
- L4 – medial lower leg and ankle
- L5 – dorsum & medial aspect of big toe
- S1 – dorsum & lateral aspect of little toe
**Tests:**
- **Light touch** – cotton wool (demo on sternum)
- **Pinprick** – neuro-tip (demo on sternum)
- **Proprioception** – move big toe up/down, eyes closed
- Assess bilaterally
- Pattern recognition: localised nerve injury, neuropathy, spinal cord lesion
## Coordination
- **Heel-to-shin test**
    - Heel on opposite knee → slide down shin → return to knee
    - Repeat 3–4 times smoothly
    - Assess bilaterally
    - Dysmetria or intention tremor → ipsilateral cerebellar pathology (CVA)
