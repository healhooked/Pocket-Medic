# **General inspection**
- Surroundings: aids (crutches, splints).
- Patient: body habitus, scars, wasting.
- Gait (already done in GALS).

**Knee inspection** (front, side, back)
- **Anterior:** scars, bruising, swelling/effusion, patella position, valgus/varus deformity, wasting.
- **Lateral:** hyperextension, fixed flexion deformity.
- **Posterior:** scars, wasting, popliteal swelling (Baker’s cyst / aneurysm).

**Palpation**
- **Temperature:** both knees, compare. ↑ temp → infection/inflammation.
- **Extended knee:** palpate patella, joint lines, tenderness.
- **Patella tap:** for effusion.
- **Flexed knee (90°):** palpate patella, joint lines, fibular head, popliteal fossa (cyst/aneurysm).

**Movement**
- **Passive ROM:** flexion (heel to buttock), extension, hyperextension.
- **Active ROM:** ask patient – flexion 0–140°, extension to 180°. Look for pain/restriction/crepitus.

**Stability tests**:
- **Cruciate ligaments:** 
	- [[Drawer test]] (ACL anterior / PCL posterior)
	- [[Lachmans Test]]
- **Collateral ligaments:**
    - [[Valgus Sign]] (MCL) – straight & 30° flex.
    - [[Varus Sign]] (LCL) – straight & 30° flex.
- Compare both knees. Abnormal = excess movement / laxity.
