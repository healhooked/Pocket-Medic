| Feature            | Details                                                                                                          |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Cause**          | Often an early and sensitive sign of **cardiopulmonary disease**.                                                |
| **Mechanism**      | During exercise, increased [[Oxygen]] demand and cardiac output reveal underlying **heart** or **lung dysfunction**. |
| System             | Examples                                                                                                         |
| **Cardiac**        | Heart failure (esp. left-sided), ischaemic heart disease, valvular disorders (e.g. aortic stenosis)              |
| **Pulmonary**      | COPD, asthma, pulmonary fibrosis, pulmonary hypertension                                                         |
| **Haematologic**   | Anaemia (reduced [[Oxygen]]-carrying capacity)                                                                       |
| **Deconditioning** | Poor fitness, often in elderly or sedentary individuals                                                          |
