> [!Causes]
> - Dopamine blockade leads to movement disorders  
>     Due to:
> - [Basal Ganglia](app://obsidian.md/Basal%20Ganglia) function (voluntary movement center)
> - Loss of dopaminergic neurons in the striatum (specifically, the substantia nigra) affecting motor control.
> 

1. **Tremors**: Involuntary shaking, often in the hands.
2. **Rigidity**: Stiffness of the muscles, leading to reduced movement.
3. **Bradykinesia**: Slowness of movement.
4. **Akathisia**: A feeling of inner restlessness and an uncontrollable need to be in constant motion.
5. **Dystonia**: Abnormal muscle contractions, leading to twisting or repetitive movements.

**Mnemonic:**
- **T**: Tremors
- **R**: Rigidity
- **A**: Akathisia
- **P**: Postural instability (or Bradykinesia)
