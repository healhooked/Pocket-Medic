### Atiology:

🫁 Pulmonary Causes (most common overall):
- Lung cancer (especially non-small cell types like adenocarcinoma)
- Bronchiectasis
- Lung abscess
- Tuberculosis
- Cystic fibrosis
- Idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis


❤️ Cardiac Causes (especially with chronic hypoxia):
- Cyanotic congenital heart disease (e.g., Tetralogy of Fallot)
- Infective endocarditis (especially subacute)
- Atrial myxoma

🧠 Gastrointestinal & Hepatic Causes:
- Inflammatory bowel disease (Crohn’s disease, ulcerative colitis)
- Cirrhosis (especially primary biliary cholangitis)
- Celiac disease


🪫 Other Rare Causes:
- Thyroid acropachy (in Graves’ disease)
- Hereditary clubbing (familial, benign)

🚫 Clubbing is not seen in:
COPD (chronic bronchitis/emphysema) — important clinical distinction
