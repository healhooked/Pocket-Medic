| **Cause**                       | **Clinical Significance**                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| *Cardiac syncope*               | ⚠️ Most concerning — indicates **arrhythmia**, **structural heart disease**, or **valvular issues** (e.g., [[Aortic Stenosis]]). |
| *Neurally mediated (vasovagal)* | Often **benign**, especially in younger individuals; triggered by pain, fear, or standing too long.                              |
| *Orthostatic hypotension*       | May signal **autonomic dysfunction**, **volume depletion**, or **medication side effects** (e.g., antihypertensives, diuretics). |
| *Neurological causes*           | Rare — consider **seizure**, **TIA**, or **stroke** if abnormal movements, postictal state, or focal deficits are present.       |
| *Psychogenic*                   | Consider in younger patients with normal workup and atypical episodes — **requires careful exclusion** of organic causes.        |
