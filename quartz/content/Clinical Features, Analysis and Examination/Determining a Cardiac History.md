#### **Focused Cardiovascular History**
1. **Presenting Complaint**  
    Ask symptom-specific questions:
    - **Chest pain:**?
	    - [[A Clinical Approach to Chest Pain]]
    - **SOB:** (Dyspnoea) ?
	    - Exercise tolerance? [[Dyspnea on exertion]]
	    - [[Orthopnoea]]?
	    - [[Paroxysmal Nocturnal Dyspnoea]]?
    - **[[Palpitations]]:** onset, duration
    - **[[Syncope]]:** frequency, triggers
    - Oedema
    - Cough
    - Dizziness/ Fainting ?
    
2. **Medical History (AMPLE)**
    
    - Allergies, medications, past medical history, last meal, events preceding
    - Social and family history related to cardiac conditions
        
3. **Risk Factor Assessment**  
    **Modifiable**
		- *Smoking*
		- *Obesity*
		- *Physical Activity*
		- *Hypertension*
		- *High Cholesterol (LDL, ApoB)*
		- *Poor Dietary Nutrition*
		- *Alcohol Consumption*
		- *Stress*
	**Non-Modifiable**
		- *Age*
		- *Sex*
		- *Genetics (e.g. Marfans)*
		- *Ethnicity*
		- *Family History*
