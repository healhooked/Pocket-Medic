# CPR Summary

| Age Group                | Compression:Ventilation Ratio                   | Hand Placement / Technique                                                  | Ventilations (with advanced airway)      | Notes (Rescuer Numbers, etc.)               |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| **Adult**                | 30:2 (single or 2 rescuers)                     | Two hands on centre of chest (lower sternum)                                | 1 breath every 6 sec (10/min)            | Rotate compressors every 2 min              |
| **Child (1 yr–puberty)** | 30:2 (single rescuer); 15:2 (2 rescuers)        | Heel of one hand (small child) or two hands (larger child)                  | 1 breath every 6 sec (10/min)            | Prefer 2-rescuer ratio (15:2) if possible   |
| **Infant (<1 yr)**       | 30:2 (single rescuer); 15:2 (2 rescuers)        | Two fingers (single rescuer) or two-thumb encircling technique (2 rescuers) | 1 breath every 6 sec (10/min)            | Two rescuers recommended for best perfusion |
| **Neonate (<28 days)**   | 3:1 (preferred if cause is primary respiratory) | Two-thumb encircling technique                                              | After every 3 compressions give 1 breath | If cardiac cause suspected, may use 15:2    |
