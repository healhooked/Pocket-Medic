# Breech Birth & Post-Birth Care Protocol

---

## **Birth Management**

- **Identify birth is imminent**  
    (Urge to Push, Bulging Perineum, Contractions < 2.5 minutes)
    
- **Instruct secondary to set up a Baby Station**
    
- **Encourage Mum**
    
- **Identify the presenting part is the buttocks → HANDS OFF**
    
- **Allow birth of the baby to the shoulders unassisted**  
    – may flex legs to deliver if position is appropriate
    
- **Perform Løvset’s maneuver to deliver shoulders**  
    – hands must be wrapped around the hips, no higher than the buttocks
    
- **Perform MSV (Modified Smellie-Veit) to deliver the head**  
    – in a J shape
    
- **Note the time of the buttock delivery**
    
- **Note the time of the head delivered**
    

---

## **Post Birth Cares: Baby**

- **Dry, Warm, Stimulate 30–60 seconds**
    
- **Clear meconium if present**
    
- **Identify the baby is blue with no tone, no cry and requires IPPV**
    
- **Cut & Clamp the cord and move the baby to the baby station**
    
- **Place baby in a plastic bag, beanie, and booties** (premature cares)
    
- **Place the baby in the baby station for IPPV**
    
- **Must achieve correct position/airway**
    
    - Initial HR **55**
        
    - **30 Seconds IPPV / Warming / Stimulate (HR Remains 55)**
        
    - **SITREP requesting CCP &/or additional crews**
        
    - **30 Seconds IPPV w O₂ & PEEP / Warming / Stimulate (HR Remains 50)**
        
    
    _Must do 60 seconds of IPPV BEFORE commencing CPR_
    
    - **Commence CPR at a rate of 3:1**  
        – HR trends upwards of 10 points from here for every good round of quality CPR/IPPV
        
    - **When HR is above 60 → Compressions cease and switch to IPPV**
        
    - **At 105 bpm baby starts to show signs of life**  
        – cry / fight the mask / breathe (approx. 5 minutes CPR/IPPV)
        
    
    _Must show signs of life before ceasing efforts even if HR is above 100_
    
- **APGAR (1 minute, 5 minutes) – can be retrospective**
    
    - APGAR at 1 minute: A-0, P-1, G-0, A-0, R-0 = **1**
        
    - APGAR at 5 minutes: A-1, P-1, G-1, A-2, R-2 = **7**

## **Post Birth Cares: Mother**

- **The immediate focus should be the delivery of placenta / Third-stage labour**
    
- **Check mum for perineal tears (NIL) & Vaginal Bleeding (NIL)**
    
- **Basic Vitals when appropriate (Minimum BP Cuff – continuous)**
    
    - BP 110/65, HR 90, RR 12, SATS 100%
        
- **Discuss Oxytocin & Third stage**
    
    - **Passive:**
        
        - Breastfeeding
            
        - Skin-to-skin
            
        - Urination
            
        - No guarding / cord traction
            
    - **Active:**
        
        - Breastfeeding
            
        - Skin-to-skin
            
        - Urination
            
        - Oxytocin IM 10 IU
            
        - Uterus guarding
            
        - Controlled cord traction
            
    - _Should respect the patient’s wishes to HAVE / NOT HAVE Oxytocin if the third stage is otherwise going well_
        
- **Additional considerations may be:**
    
    - Position (squatting / sitting on the toilet)
        
    - Warm / quiet / dark environment
        
    - Music (anything that promotes the natural production of oxytocin)
