***Differential Considerations:***
***[[Cauda Equina Syndrome]]***
Severe back pain, motor weakness, sensory changes, bowel/bladder dysfunction.
Action: Assess for neurological deficits and transport urgently.

***Metastatic Cancer***
Sudden, severe back pain, especially in patients with a cancer history; possible neurologic symptoms.
Action: Evaluate for signs of spinal instability or fractures and transport to an oncology center if indicated.

***Spinal Epidural Abscess:***
Nonspecific initial symptoms (fever, malaise), progressing to localized back pain and neurologic deficits.
Action: Recognize the potential for serious infection and ensure rapid transport to a facility capable of managing surgical interventions.

***Vertebral Osteomyelitis:***
Key Symptoms: Gradually increasing back pain, possible fever, positional discomfort, pain to palpation, neurologic signs.
Action: Identify risk factors and ensure timely transport for diagnostic imaging and treatment.
