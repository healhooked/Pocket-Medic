
*Causes:*
1. *Medications:*
    - [[Antidepressants]]: Selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors ([[SSRIs]]), serotonin-norepinephrine reuptake inhibitors ([[SNRIs]]), and monoamine oxidase inhibitors (MAOIs).
    - Opioids: Certain opioids like [[Tramadol]] and [[Fentanyl]]
    - Migraine medications: Triptans (e.g., sumatriptan).
    - Stimulants: Amphetamines and certain [[ADHD]] medications.
    - Herbal supplements: St. John's Wort and others that may increase serotonin levels.

3. *Illicit Drugs:*

    - Certain medical conditions that affect serotonin metabolism or clearance, such as liver disease.

Diagnostic Criteria

Hunter Serotonin Toxicity Criteria:
1. **Recent use of a serotonergic agent**: This can include medications such as [[SSRIs]], SNRIs, MAOIs, certain opioids, and illicit drugs.
    
2. **Presence of specific symptoms**: The diagnosis is supported by the presence of at least one of the following:
    
    - Spontaneous clonus
    - Inducible clonus (with agitation or diaphoresis)
    - Ocular clonus (with agitation or diaphoresis)
    - Tremor and hyperreflexia
    - Hypertonia (muscle rigidity) and temperature >38°C (100.4°F)
    - Agitation or confusion

**Clinical Presentation**

4. **Neuromuscular Symptoms**: 5HT2A & 5HT3 Involved in neuromsuclar function, abnormal rapid firing of neurons.
   - Hyperreflexia (exaggerated reflexes)
   - Tremor
   - Clonus (involuntary muscle contractions)
   - Muscle rigidity
   - Myoclonus (sudden muscle jerks)

2. **Autonomic Symptoms**: 5HT in the ANS, catecholamines.
   - Hyperthermia (elevated body temperature)
   - Sweating
   - Tachycardia (increased heart rate)
   - Hypertension (high blood pressure), 5HT2A in blood vessels.
   - Diarrhea
   - Nausea and vomiting

1. **Cognitive and Behavioral Symptoms**: Rapid changes in monoamines.
   - Agitation
   - Confusion
   - Hallucinations
   - Delirium

**Treatment:
1. **Discontinuation of Serotonergic Medications**: Stop all medications that may contribute to serotonin syndrome.
    
2. **Supportive Care**:
    
    - **Monitoring**: Continuous monitoring of vital signs, including heart rate, blood pressure, and temperature.
    - **Cooling Measures**: If [[🌡️Hyperthermia]] is present, use cooling blankets or ice packs to reduce body temperature.
3. **Symptomatic Treatment**:
    - **Benzodiazepines**: Medications like lorazepam or [[Diazepam]] can help manage agitation, anxiety, and muscle rigidity.
    - **Serotonin Antagonists**: In severe cases, medications such as cyproheptadine (an antihistamine with serotonin-blocking properties) may be used.

4. **Supportive Measures**: Ensure hydration and electrolyte balance, and provide intravenous fluids if necessary.
    
5. **Severe Cases**: In cases of severe serotonin syndrome with complications (e.g., seizures, severe autonomic instability), hospitalization and intensive care may be required.
