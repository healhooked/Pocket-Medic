## ***Consider - Reversible Causes***
#### *Metabolic disturbances*
- [[Hypoglycemia]] (low blood sugar)
- [[Hyperglycemia]] (especially non-ketotic hyperosmolar states)

#### *Electrolyte disturbances*
- [[Hyponatremia]] (low sodium),[[Hypernatremia]]
- [[Hypocalcemia]]
- [[Hypomagnesemia]]

- Uremia (from renal failure)
- Hepatic encephalopathy

#### *Toxins and drugs*
- Withdrawal - ([[Alcoholism - SUD]]])
- Withdrawal - ([[Sedatives & Anxiolytics - SUD]]) (e.g. benzodiazepines)
- [[Stimulant - SUD]], & Illicit Drug Use (e.g., cocaine, amphetamines, ecstasy)
- Drug toxicity (e.g., from [[Tramadol]], isoniazid, theophylline, [[Lithium]])
- [[Organophosphate Poisoning]] (e.g., lead, organophosphates)

#### *Infections*
- Meningitis ([[Meningococcal Meningitis]])
- Encephalopathy ([[Wernicke's Encephalopathy]]) 
- [[SEPSIS]]
- Neurocysticercosis (in endemic regions)
- Malaria (cerebral malaria)

#### *Structural brain lesions (potentially reversible)*
- Tumors (if surgically resectable)
- Brain abscess
- Acute [[Stroke & or Transient Ischaemic Attack]] or hemorrhage (seizures may resolve once the acute phase passes or is treated)
#### *[[Trauma]]*
- Post-traumatic seizures in the acute period after head injury (especially within the first week),  [[Traumatic Brain Injury 🧠]]

#### *Risk Factors*
- Sleep deprivation

#### *Hormonal fluctuations*
- Eclampsia (in pregnancy)
- Catamenial seizures (menstrual cycle-related)

*[[Common Epilepsy Controlling Medications]]*
- **Midazolam / Diazepam**  
    ↳ **First-line for acute seizures and status epilepticus**.  
    ↳ Fast-acting benzodiazepines (IM, IV, or buccal routes).
- **Sodium Valproate (Epilim)**  
    ↳ Broad-spectrum, especially for generalised seizures.  
    ↳ Avoid in pregnancy due to high teratogenicity.
- **Levetiracetam (Keppra)**  
    ↳ Broad-spectrum. Minimal side effects and drug interactions.  
    ↳ Common first-line, especially in emergency or add-on therapy.
- **Lamotrigine**  
    ↳ Broad-spectrum. Often used long-term.  
    ↳ Safer in pregnancy. Watch for rash.
- **Carbamazepine**  
    ↳ First-line for **focal seizures**.  
    ↳ Enzyme inducer — many drug interactions.
