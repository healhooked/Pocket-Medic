## 🏠 **Functional Assessment: ADLs & IADLs**

Assess when there is an **acute, progressive, or chronic functional problem**

---
### ✅ **Basic ADLs (Activities of Daily Living)**

- **Walking (Ambulating)** – Moving around inside or outside the home
- **Feeding** – Getting food from plate to mouth
- **Dressing & Grooming** – Choosing clothes, putting them on, maintaining appearance
- **Toileting** – Getting to/from toilet, using it, cleaning afterward
- **Bathing** – Washing face and body in bath/shower
- **Transferring** – Moving between positions (e.g., bed to chair)

---
### 🛠 **Instrumental ADLs (iADLs)**

- **Managing finances** – Paying bills, handling money
- **Managing transportation** – Driving or arranging other transport
- **Shopping & meal prep** – Buying food/clothes; cooking meals
- **Housekeeping & maintenance** – Cleaning, tidying, maintaining home
- **Managing communication** – Using phone, mail, computers
- **Managing medications** – Getting and taking medications as prescribed
