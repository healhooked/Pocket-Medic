The Rule of 4s

---
## History and General Observation
- Ask relevant neurological review of systems questions
- Perform general inspection:
    - Speech abnormalities
    - Facial asymmetry
    - Eyelid abnormalities
    - Limb inspection
    - Observe surroundings for aids

---
## Cranial Nerve Examination
## I Olfactory Nerve
- Ask about sense of smell and taste?

## II Optic Nerve
- [[Test visual acuity]]
- [[Assess visual fields]]

## III, IV VI - Oculomotor, Trochlear & Abducens Nerves
- Inspect pupils (shape, size, symmetry)
- Test direct pupillary reflex
- Test consensual pupillary reflex
- Assess all eye movements
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4e7vcvDgHw

## V Trigeminal Nerve 
- Test facial sensation (touch, pain)
- Assess jaw movement (motor function)

## VII Facial Nerve
- Test taste (anterior two-thirds of tongue)
- Assess facial muscle movements (smile, frown, puff cheeks, close eyes tightly, show teeth)

## VIII Vestibulocochlear Nerve 
- Gross hearing assessment (e.g., finger rub, whisper test)
- Perform Romberg's test (for balance)

## IX & X Glossopharyngeal and Vagus Nerve 

- Assess swallowing
- Observe uvula and soft palate for movement
- Listen to quality of speech

## XI Accessory Nerve

- Test shoulder movement (shrug against resistance)
- Test head/neck movement (turn head against resistance)

## VII Hypoglossal Nerve
- Inspect tongue at rest and on protrusion
- Assess tongue movement (side to side, pushing against cheek)
