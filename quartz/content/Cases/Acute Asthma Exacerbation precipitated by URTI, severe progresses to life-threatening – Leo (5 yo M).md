## Assessment Rubric: Acute exacerbation of asthma, severe progresses to life-threatening.
**1.**       **Appropriate primary and secondary survey assessment.**
1.1    In the first few minutes (< 8 minutes from AVPU assessment), the learner should complete a primary and secondary survey, history, and chest exam, and be able to reach an accurate working diagnosis: **_Asthma, severe progresses to life-threatening_**, based on the following:
i.     General impression (increased work of breathing).
ii.    History (known asthmatic, history of URTI symptoms).
iii.   Hypoxia (Cyanosed mucosa, Vitals and SaO2 < 90%).
iv.    Chest exam (low-pitched insp/expiratory wheeze, accessory muscle use, resp distress).
1.2    The learner should rule out other causes of respiratory distress (i.e. epiglottitis, croup, etc.).
