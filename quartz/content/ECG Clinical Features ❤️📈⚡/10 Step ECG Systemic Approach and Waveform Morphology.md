***Rhythm***
- Regular or irregular. 
- Identify the origin of the rhythm (e.g., sinus, atrial, junctional, ventricular).

***Heart Rate:*** 
-  R-R intervals in a specific time frame or using the rule of 300s.
- ![[Pasted image 20250512220555.png]]

***P Waves:***
 - 2 boxes across, one box up.
- x1 P wave per QRS complex
- Examine for asymmetry, consider atrial enlargement?

***PR Interval***: 
- Normal) 3 to 5 boxes
- Conduction from the atria to the ventricles. 
- Beginning of P wave

***QRS Complex***: 
- Normal QRS duration is less than 3 boxes

***ST Segment***
- Consider the isoelectric line
- STD or Elevation > 1 small box ?

***T Waves***: 
- Shape and direction. 
- Inverted T waves ?
- [[Hyperacute T waves]]

***QT* Interval**: 
- Normal) 8-11 Boxes (~2 big boxes)
- Measure the QT interval and correct it for heart rate
- A prolonged QT interval can increase the risk of arrhythmias.

***QTc:***
- The time it takes for the heart's electrical system to reset after each heartbeat, adjusted for heart rate.
- A prolonged QTc can indicate an increased risk of arrhythmias. It is commonly calculated using Bazett's formula: QTc = QT / √(RR).
- For example, a QTc of 520 ms would be considered high and could warrant further evaluation and monitoring.
