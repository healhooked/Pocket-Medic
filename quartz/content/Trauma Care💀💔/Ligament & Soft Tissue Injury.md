*🦶 Ankle*
- **Lateral Ankle Sprain** (ATFL – Anterior Talofibular Ligament)
- Most common ligament injury in the body.
- **Deltoid Ligament Sprain**
    - Less common, often from eversion injury.
- **High Ankle Sprain** (Syndesmosis injury)
    - Injury to tibiofibular ligaments; slower to heal.

---
*🦵 Knee*
- **ACL Tear** (Anterior Cruciate Ligament), Common in twisting injuries (sports like soccer, skiing).
- **MCL Sprain / Tear** (Medial Collateral Ligament), Blow to lateral side of the knee.
- **Meniscus Tear** (Medial or Lateral), Often with twisting; medial more commonly injured.
- **LCL / PCL Injury**, Less common but can occur in high-impact trauma.

***Ligament Tear Management Guide:***

| **Grade** | **Tear Severity**   | **Stability**     | **Swelling** | **Management**           |
| --------- | ------------------- | ----------------- | ------------ | ------------------------ |
| I         | Micro-tears/stretch | Stable            | Mild         | RICE, early mobility     |
| II        | Partial tear        | Slightly unstable | Moderate     | Brace, physio            |
| III       | Complete rupture    | Unstable          | Severe       | Surgery often considered |

*🤲 Wrist & Hand*
- **Scapholunate Ligament Injury**
    
    - Common after a fall on an outstretched hand.
        
- **TFCC Tear** (Triangular Fibrocartilage Complex)
    
    - Pain on ulnar side of wrist.
        
- **Thumb UCL Injury** ("Skier’s Thumb")
    
    - Tear of ulnar collateral ligament of the thumb MCP joint.

____________
 *💪 Shoulder*
- **Rotator Cuff Tear** (esp. Supraspinatus)
    
    - Common with overhead movements and aging.
        
- **AC Joint Sprain** (Acromioclavicular Joint)
    
    - Often from a fall directly on the shoulder.
        
- **Labral Tear** (SLAP lesion)
    
    - Injury to the ring of cartilage around the glenoid.
        

---
*💃 Hip & Groin*

- **Adductor Strain**
    - Common in sports requiring cutting or sudden changes in direction.
- **Hip Flexor Strain** (e.g., Iliopsoas)
    - Often due to sprinting or kicking.
- **Hamstring Strain**
    - Sudden sprinting or overstretching.
- **Gluteal Tendinopathy**
    - Often in runners and women >40.
---

### 💥 Other Notables
- **Achilles Tendon Rupture**
       - Sudden “pop” in the calf area; often in middle-aged recreational athletes.
      
- **Plantar Fasciitis**
       - Chronic microtears and inflammation of the plantar fascia.
      
- **IT Band Syndrome**
       - Lateral knee pain in runners.
