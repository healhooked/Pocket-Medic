#### Hyperacute T waves (HATW)

Broad, asymmetrically peaked or ‘_hyperacute_’ T-waves (HATW) are seen in the early stages of ST-elevation MI (STEMI), and often precede the appearance of ST elevation and Q waves. Particular attention should be paid to their size in relation to the preceding QRS complex, as HATW may appear ‘normal’ in size if the preceding QRS complex is of a small amplitude.

They are also seen with Prinzmetal angina.
