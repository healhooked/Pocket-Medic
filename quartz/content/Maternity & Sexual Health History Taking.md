
**Menstrual History:**
• When was your last menstrual period (LMP)?
• Are your menstrual cycles regular or irregular?
• Do you experience dysmenorrhea (painful periods) or menorrhagia (heavy menstrual bleeding)?

**Obstetric History:**
• How many pregnancies have you had (gravida)?
• How many live births, miscarriages, or terminations (parity)?
• Any history of ectopic pregnancies or complications during previous pregnancies?

**Gynecological History:**
• Have you had any recent gynecological procedures (e.g., dilation and curettage)?
• Any history of pelvic inflammatory disease (PID) or sexually transmitted infections (STIs)?
• When was your last Pap smear, and what were the results?

**Sexual History:**
• Are you currently sexually active?
• What type of contraception are you using, if any?
• Have you had any recent changes in sexual partners or practices?
• Any history of STIs or symptoms suggestive of an STI (e.g., discharge, pain during intercourse)?

**Medical and Surgical History:**
• Do you have any chronic medical conditions (e.g., coagulopathies, thyroid disease)?
• Are you taking any medications, including over-the-counter or complementary medicines?
• Any history of surgeries, particularly gynecological surgeries?

**Family History:**
• Any family history of gynecological conditions, such as endometriosis or fibroids?
• Any family history of bleeding disorders?

**Lifestyle Factors:**
• Do you smoke or use tobacco products?
• What is your alcohol consumption like?
