Buprenorphine is a medication used primarily in the treatment of opioid dependence and pain management. It is a partial agonist at the mu-opioid receptor, which means it activates the receptor but to a lesser degree than full agonists like morphine or heroin.

### Mechanism of Action:
- **Partial Agonist**: Buprenorphine partially activates mu-opioid receptors, providing pain relief and reducing withdrawal symptoms without producing the full effects of stronger opioids.
- **Ceiling Effect**: It has a ceiling effect on respiratory depression, making it safer in terms of overdose risk compared to full agonists.
- **Opioid Antagonist Properties**: At higher doses, it can block the effects of other opioids.

### Indication:
Buprenorphine is indicated for the treatment of opioid use disorder and for managing moderate to severe pain.

### Drug Class:
Buprenorphine belongs to the drug class known as **opioid partial agonists**.

### Side Effects:
- Nausea
- Constipation
- Headache
- Dizziness
- Sedation
- Sweating
