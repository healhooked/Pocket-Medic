### Mechanism of Action of Clotiapine

#### I. Overview
   - Atypical antipsychotic for schizophrenia treatment.

#### II. Neurotransmitter Interactions
   A. Dopamine Antagonism
      - Primarily targets D2 receptors.
   B. Serotonin Modulation
      - Antagonizes 5-HT2A receptors.

#### III. Additional Receptor Effects
   A. Adrenergic
      - Antagonizes α1-adrenergic receptors.
   B. Histamine
      - Antagonizes H1 receptors.

#### IV. Clinical Implications
   - Reduces schizophrenia symptoms; potential side effects include sedation and metabolic changes.
