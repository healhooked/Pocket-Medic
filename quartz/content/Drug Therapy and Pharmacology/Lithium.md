Indications: 
- Bipolar Disorder
- Major Depressive Disorder

**Mechanism of Action**
   A. Mood Stabilization
      1. Modulation of neurotransmitter systems (serotonin, norepinephrine, dopamine)
      2. Inhibition of inositol monophosphatase

   B. Neuroprotective Effects
      1. Promotion of neurogenesis
      2. Enhancement of synaptic plasticity
   
   C. Regulation of Second Messenger Systems
      3. Impact on phosphoinositide signaling
      4. Influence on cyclic AMP (cAMP) pathways

#### III. Side Effects Related to Mechanism
   A. Neurological Effects
      1. Tremors
      2. Cognitive impairment
   B. Endocrine Effects
      3. Thyroid dysfunction (hypothyroidism)
   C. Renal Effects
      2. Nephrogenic diabetes insipidus
   D. Gastrointestinal Effects
      2. Nausea and diarrhea
