### Mechanism of Action:
- **Alpha-2 Adrenergic Agonist**: Lofexidine stimulates alpha-2 receptors in the brain, which reduces the release of norepinephrine and helps alleviate withdrawal symptoms such as anxiety, agitation, and sweating.

### Indication:
- Lofexidine is indicated for the management of opioid withdrawal symptoms in individuals who are detoxifying from opioids.

### Drug Class:
- **Alpha-2 Adrenergic Agonists**.

### Side Effects:
- Drowsiness
- Dry mouth
- Hypotension (low blood pressure)
- Bradycardia (slow heart rate)
- Fatigue
- Nausea
