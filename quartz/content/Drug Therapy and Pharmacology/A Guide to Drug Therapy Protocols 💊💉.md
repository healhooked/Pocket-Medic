**🫁 *Airway & Respiratory Support***
- **[[Adrenaline]]**  – [[Anaphylaxis]], [[Asthma 🐡]], [[Cardiac Arrest & Resuscitation]].
- **[[Sodium Chloride 0.9%]]**– Nebulized for [[Croup 🐣🐤]]; IV fluid support.

- **[[Oxygen]]** – Hypoxia, respiratory distress.
- **[[Ipratropium Bromide]]** – Bronchodilator in [[Asthma 🐡]]/[[COPD 🚬]].
- **[[Salbutamol]] –** Beta-agonist bronchodilator, [[Asthma 🐡]], [[COPD 🚬]]
- **[[Hydrocortisone]]** – [[Asthma 🐡]] exacerbation, [[Adrenal Insufficiency - Addisonian Crisis]].

***Extended Scope***:
	- **[[Hypertonic Saline 7.5%]]** – Raised intracranial pressure.
	- **[[Magnesium Sulphate]]** – Severe [[Asthma 🐡]], eclampsia.

💉 ***Analgesia & Sedation***
- **[[Fentanyl]]** – Potent opioid analgesic.
- **[[Morphine]]** – Opioid analgesic for severe pain.
- **[[Methoxyflurane]]** – Inhaled analgesic.

- **[[Midazolam]]** – Sedation, seizures.

- **[[Paracetamol]]** – Mild/moderate pain, fever.
- **[[Ibuprofen]]** – Pain, inflammation. 

- **[[Droperidol]]** – Sedation, antiemetic.
- **[[Olanzapine]]** – [[Acute Behavioural Disturbance (ABD)]]

- **[[Sucrose 24%]]** – Neonatal analgesia.

***Extended Scope:***
	- **[[Ketamine]]** – Dissociative analgesic, sedation.
	- **[[Lorazepam]]** – Status epilepticus.
	- **[[Morphine and Midazolam]] (combined)** – Palliative, critical care.
	- **[[Fentanyl and Midazolam]] (combined)** – Procedural sedation.

---
***❤️ [[Cardiac Arrest & Resuscitation]] & Arrhythmias***
- **[[Amiodarone]]** – VF/pulseless VT.

***Extended Scope:***
	- **[[Isoprenaline]]** – Bradycardia (temporary pacing adjunct).
	- [[Vasopressin]] – Alternative vasopressor (advanced settings).
	- [[Lidocaine 1%]] – Alternative antiarrhythmic/local anesthetic.
	- [[Atropine]] – Bradycardia.
	- [[Metoprolol]] – Tachyarrhythmias.
	- [[Noradrenaline]] – Hypotension/[[Shock]].

***🩸 Cardiovascular Support & Thrombolysis***
- **[[Aspirin]] -**  [[Acute Coronary Syndrome (ACS)]], [[Unstable Angina & the NSTEMI]]
- **[[Glyceryl Trinitrate (GTN)]]** – [[Acute Coronary Syndrome (ACS)]], [[Unstable Angina & the NSTEMI]]

- **[[Heparin]]** – [[pPCI Referral]]
- **[[Clopidogrel]]** – [[pPCI Referral]], [[Fibrinolysis]]
- **[[Ticagrelor]]** – [[pPCI Referral]]

- **[[Enoxaparin]]** – [[Fibrinolysis]]
- **[[Tenecteplase]]** – [[Fibrinolysis]]

***Extended Scope:***
	- **[[Metaraminol]] –** Vasopressor for hypotension.
	- **[[Hydralazine]] –** Severe hypertension (obstetric use).
	- **[[Sodium Bicarbonate 8.4%]] –** [[Tricyclic Antidepressants (TCAs) & Overdose]], [[Hyperkalaemia 🍌]].
	**- [[Calcium Gluconate 10%]]** – [[Hyperkalaemia 🍌]], calcium channel blocker overdose.


---
***🧠 Neurological / Seizure Management***
- **[[Midazolam]]** – First-line for seizures.
- **[[Glucose 10%, IV]]** – [[Hypoglycemia]].
- **[[Glucagon]]** – [[Hypoglycemia]] when no IV access.

***Extended Scope:***
	**- [[Insulin (Short-acting – Actrapid®)]]** – [[Hyperkalaemia 🍌]], [[Diabetic Emergencies]]
	**- [[Levetiracetam]] –** Antiepileptic.
	**- [[Lorazepam]] –** Benzodiazepine for seizures.

***🧬 Endocrine & Metabolic***
- **[[Dexamethasone]]** – Steroid use for [[Croup 🐣🐤]], [[Asthma 🐡]], etc.
- **[[Hydrocortisone]]** – [[Adrenal Insufficiency - Addisonian Crisis]], [[Anaphylaxis]]. 

- **[[Glucagon]]** – [[Hypoglycemia]].
- **[[Glucose ORAL]]** – Conscious [[Hypoglycemia]].
- **[[Glucose 10%, IV]]** – Unconscious [[Hypoglycemia]].

***Extended Scope:***

- **[[Droperidol]]** – Anti-emetic, sedation.

***Extended Scope:***
	- **[[Loperamide]]** – Diarrhea (rare in prehospital use).
