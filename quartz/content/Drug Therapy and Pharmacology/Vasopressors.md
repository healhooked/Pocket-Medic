1. **Septic Shock**: To counteract severe hypotension caused by sepsis and improve blood flow to organs.
2. **Cardiogenic Shock**: To support blood pressure and cardiac output in patients with heart failure or after a heart attack.
3. **Hypovolemic Shock**: To stabilize blood pressure in cases of significant fluid loss (e.g., from trauma or severe dehydration) while fluid resuscitation is ongoing.
4. **Anaphylactic Shock**: To treat severe allergic reactions that cause a drop in blood pressure.
5. **Postoperative Hypotension**: To manage low blood pressure following surgery, especially in high-risk patients.
6. **Neurogenic Shock**: To support blood pressure in cases of spinal cord injury leading to loss of sympathetic tone.
