1. ***Purpose of EEA:*** 
Authorizes temporary detention and transport of a person to a treatment or care place when they are at immediate risk of serious harm due to a major disturbance in mental capacity. 🚨  
2. ***Criteria for EEA:***  
• Person’s behaviour indicates immediate risk of serious harm ⚠️  
• Risk appears to result from a major disturbance in mental capacity  
• Person requires urgent examination, treatment, or care for the disturbance 🏥  

3. ***Determining capacity:***  
• Assess clinical findings, observations, and discussions with the patient 🧠  
• Consider relevant [[information]] from family members or other sources  
• Look for signs such as:  
• Threatening suicide 😢  
• Acts or threats of self-harm  
• Reckless behavior endangering self or others  
• Deterioration in mental state  
• Symptoms of mania, [[Psychosis Disorders]], or other behavioral disturbances  
• Aberrant behavior compromising patient safety  

4. ***Transport and detention:***  
• Inform patient of detention and explain implications 📢  
• Transport to a Public Sector Health Service (PSHS) facility or appropriate treatment place  
• Use necessary and reasonable force if required  

5. ***Documentation:***  
• Complete QAS Electronic Ambulance Report Form (eARF) 📝  
• Fill out Queensland Government EEA form for PSHS facilities  
• Record reasons for forming belief that EEA criteria were met  

6. ***Examination period:***  
• Up to 6 hours at PSHS facility, extendable to 12 hours if necessary ⏳  

7. ***Consultation:***  
• Consult QAS Mental Health Liaison Service or Clinical Consultation and Advice Line for non-PSHS destinations  
This procedure emphasizes the importance of careful assessment and documentation when determining a patient’s capacity and need for emergency examination and treatment. 📋
