### [[Chronotropes]]
(These affect [[Heart Rate]])

- **Positive [[Chronotropes]]**: Increase [[Heart Rate]]
    - Examples: Atropine, Isoproterenol
- **Negative [[Chronotropes]]**: Decrease [[Heart Rate]]
    - Examples: Beta-blockers (e.g., Metoprolol), Digoxin
### [[Inotropics]]
(These affect the force of heart contractions)

- **Positive Inotropes**: Increase the force of contraction
    - Examples: Dobutamine, Dopamine, Milrinone, Digoxin
- **Negative Inotropes**: Decrease the force of contraction
    - Examples: Beta-blockers (e.g., Metoprolol), Calcium channel blockers (e.g., Verapamil)
