***Arrythmias Associated with Palpitations:***
- [[Supraventricular Tachycardia (SVT)]]
- [[Atrial Fibrillation]]
- [[Premature Ventricular Contractions (PVCs)]]
- [[Premature Atrial Contractions (PACs)]]
- [[Atrial Flutter]]
- [[Ventricular Tachycardia (VT)]]


| Feature                          | Details                                                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **May Indicate Arrhythmia**      | Palpitations can be a symptom of **serious cardiac arrhythmias**, including atrial fibrillation, SVT, VT, or even ventricular fibrillation in rare cases. |
| **Red Flag for Cardiac Disease** | When associated with **chest pain, syncope, dizziness, or exertion**, palpitations may indicate **structural heart disease or ischemia**.                 |
| **Autonomic Dysregulation**      | Common in **anxiety, panic disorders**, and **POTS (Postural Orthostatic Tachycardia Syndrome)** — especially in younger patients.                        |
| **Hormonal Causes**              | Can reflect **thyrotoxicosis**, **pheochromocytoma**, or **menopausal hormone changes**.                                                                  |
| **Stimulant Use**                | Linked to **caffeine, nicotine, recreational drugs (e.g. cocaine, amphetamines)**, or some medications (e.g. salbutamol, pseudoephedrine).                |
| **Electrolyte Imbalance**        | Hypokalaemia, hypomagnesaemia, or hypocalcaemia can trigger palpitations and arrhythmias.                                                                 |
