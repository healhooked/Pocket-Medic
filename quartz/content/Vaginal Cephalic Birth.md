Labour is defined as the process by which the fetus, placenta and membranes are expelled from the birth canal

Normal birth is defined by the World Health Organisation as:
- spontaneous in onset 
- low-risk at the start of labour 
- remaining low-risk throughout labour and birth 
- the newborn is born: - spontaneously - in the vertex position - between 37 and 42 completed weeks gestation 
- after birth, the woman and newborn are in good condition

### Signs of Imminent Birth:
- Mother states baby has dropped in position
- Loss of operculum plug (mucous plug and bloody show)
- Increasing frequency and severity of contractions
- Urge to push or open bowels
- Spontaneous rupture of Membrane (SROM - may not always occur)
- Bulging perineum
- Appearance of presenting part of vulva

# The Stages of Labour
## Stage 1
The Latent First Stage
- painful uterine contractions and variable changes of the cervix
- Effacement and slower progression of dilation up to 5 cm

The Active First Stage
- Regular Painful uterine contractions
- Cervical effacement and more rapid crvical dilation from 5 cm - 10 cm
______________________
### Stage 2
- Full Cervical dilatation
- Involuntary urge to bear down, expulsive uterine contractions
- Varies on an individual basis
- Usually completed in 3 hours in subsequent; labours 2 hours
______________________
## Stage 3
- Explusion fo placenta and membranes
- The uterus gently contracts to loosen and push out the placenta
- 5 - 30 mins after the birth of the baby
- Muscles of the uterus continue to contract

**Management:** 
- IM Oxytocin adminstraiton (ensure consent & after final fetus)
- Look for sings of placental separation
- Apply gentle traction on the cord
- Once placental bed is in view, gently roll the placenta in an effort to not to tear the membraines or placental bed.
- If the placenta is brithed check the integrity and transport to hsopital
- Uterine tonus assessment (uterus should be firm and central)
- If not firm, fundal massage may be required post placental birth
- EBL - remember to expect ~250 mls (>500 mls PPH)

## Care for the Newborn:
Intial Tactile Stimulation of newborn
Ensure clear airway is patent (suction only if required)
Newborns become ambient FAST keep them warm
Record APGAR score - 1 mins and 5 mins
Encourage skin to skin contact
Encourage breast feeding (ensure it is what mother wishes to do)
Delayed cord clamping (~5 minutes or until cord is no longer pulsatile)

*If neonates born through clear amniotic fluid who start breathing on their own after birth, suctioning of the mouth and nose should not be performed*
