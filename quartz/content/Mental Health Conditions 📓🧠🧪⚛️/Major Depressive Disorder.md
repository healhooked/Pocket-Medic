***Diagnostic Criteria:***
At least 5 symptoms for 2+ weeks, including 
(1) depressed mood; or 
(2) loss of interest/pleasure:
1. [ ] Depressed mood most of the day
2. [ ] Loss of interest or pleasure in most activities
3. [ ] Weight/appetite change
4. [ ] Insomnia or hypersomnia
5. [ ] Psychomotor agitation or slowing
6. [ ] Fatigue or low energy
7. [ ] Feelings of worthlessness or guilt
8. [ ] Trouble thinking, concentrating, or making decisions
9. [ ] Thoughts of death or suicide
