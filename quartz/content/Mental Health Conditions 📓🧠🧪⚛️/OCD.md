
> [!note] Identifying Features
> - Obsessions, intrusive, unwanted thoughts (e.g. fear of germs)
> - Compulsions, repetitive behaviours to reduce anxiety (e.g. excessive handwashing)


***[[Field Reference Pathophysiology]]***
- **CSTC Circuit Dysfunction**:
    - Hyperactivity in [[orbitofrontal cortex]](OFC) and [[Anterior cingulate cortex]]
    - (ACC).
    - Hypoactivity in caudate nucleus.
- [[Monoamine Dysfunction]]
    - **Serotonin**: Reduced levels → obsessions/compulsions.
    - **Dopamine**: Increased activity → compulsive behaviors.
- **Genetic Factors**:
    - Heritable component; specific gene involvement.
- **[[Epigenetics]]
- Environmental stressors affect gene expression.

**Treatment:
- [[Psychotherapy]]
- [[SSRIs]]
- [[Tricyclic Antidepressants (TCAs) & Overdose]]
- Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation
- Deep Brain Stimulation
