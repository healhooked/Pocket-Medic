- Disregard for others' rights.
    
- **DSM-5 Criteria**:
    
    - Age ≥ 18.
    - History of conduct disorder < 15.
    - Symptoms:
        - Nonconformity.
        - Deceitfulness.
        - Impulsivity.
        - Aggressiveness.
        - Recklessness.
        - Irresponsibility.
        - Lack of remorse.
- **Prevalence**: More common in males; often with substance use disorders.
    
- **Etiology**: Genetic, environmental, neurobiological factors.
    
- **Treatment**: Limited; focus on behavioral therapy and managing co-occurring issues.
