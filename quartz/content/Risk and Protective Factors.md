# ⚠️ Risk and Protective Factors for Suicide

## 🔴 Risk Factors
Risk factors increase the likelihood of suicidal behaviour. Tick those present:
- [ ] Prior suicide attempt(s)  
- [ ] Alcohol and/or other drug misuse and abuse  
- [ ] Mental ill-health (e.g., mood disorder, schizophrenia, substance disorder)  
- [ ] Access to lethal means  
- [ ] Sense of hopelessness  
- [ ] Relationship breakdowns  
- [ ] Unemployment or financial stress  
- [ ] Bereavement or bereavement after suicide  
- [ ] Social isolation  
- [ ] Chronic disease or chronic pain  
- [ ] Childhood and/or adult trauma  
- [ ] Imprisonment/incarceration  
- [ ] Exposure to suicidal behaviour of others  
- [ ] Exposure to sensational media reporting about suicide deaths

---

## 🛡 Protective Factors
Protective factors reduce the likelihood of suicidal behaviours. Tick those present:
- [ ] Employment  
- [ ] Social support & connectedness in stable relationships  
- [ ] Children under 18 living at home  
- [ ] Balanced physical health  
- [ ] Plans for the future, engaging in meaningful activities & sense of purpose  
- [ ] Strong reasons for living  
- [ ] Access & availability to effective mental health care  
- [ ] Life skills (problem solving, coping skills, adaptability)  
- [ ] Cultural beliefs that discourage suicide  
- [ ] Religious beliefs that discourage suicide
