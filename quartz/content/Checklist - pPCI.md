## ✅  PPCI Referral Checklist
- [ ] **Suspected STEMI – confirm eligibility**
  - [ ] Age ≥ 18 years
  - [ ] Chest pain consistent with ACS
  - [ ] Symptom onset < 12 hours (consider up to 24 h if ongoing pain + ECG criteria)
  - [ ] ECG shows:
    - [ ] ≥1 mm ST elevation in ≥2 contiguous limb leads
    - [ ] or ≥2 mm in ≥2 contiguous precordial leads
    - [ ] or new/presumed new LBBB (rarely used; confirm with PCI team)
  - [ ] Patient not in cardiac arrest

- [ ] **Exclusions / contraindications (check and document)**
  - [ ] Advanced directive / palliative intent
  - [ ] Active bleeding / bleeding disorder
  - [ ] Severe frailty, immobility, or unable to lie flat
  - [ ] Limited life expectancy due to comorbidity
  - [ ] Cardiogenic shock needing immediate resus (may still be candidate — discuss)
  - [ ] Patient refusal

- [ ] **Prepare for referral**
  - [ ] Patient name, age, estimated weight
  - [ ] Time of symptom onset
  - [ ] 12-lead ECG (time taken + copy to transmit)
  - [ ] BP, HR, SpO₂
  - [ ] Current meds & allergies
  - [ ] Significant past medical history (CABG, PCI, bleeding risk, renal impairment)

- [ ] **Contact PPCI Coordinator / Interventional Cardiologist**
  - [ ] Identify yourself & service (QAS)
  - [ ] State suspected STEMI & that patient meets criteria
  - [ ] Provide patient details:
    - [ ] Name, age
    - [ ] Symptom onset time & ECG time
    - [ ] ECG findings (leads & magnitude)
    - [ ] Haemodynamic status
    - [ ] ETA to PCI hospital
  - [ ] Confirm acceptance & destination (direct to cath lab or ED)

- [ ] **En route care**
  - [ ] Give aspirin 300 mg PO
  - [ ] GTN if indicated and not contraindicated
  - [ ] Oxygen if SpO₂ < 94%
  - [ ] Analgesia per protocol
  - [ ] Continuous cardiac monitoring
  - [ ] Prepare defib / resus equipment

- [ ] **Before arrival**
  - [ ] Update PPCI team / hospital if ETA changes
  - [ ] Ensure ECG and paperwork are ready
  - [ ] Prepare structured clinical handover
